# Recurring AI character mini-series  (content, 20/25, 2026-10-30)

## Demand
- Viewers binge short dramas with the same silly characters coming back episode after episode. One AI fruit-character "reality show" account was reported at 300 million views and 3.3 million followers in ten days in March 2026, and a similar account at 3.1 million followers in 9 days, averaging about 15 million views per video. Source: [Daily Dot](https://lede-v2.dailydot.com/ai-slop-videos-of-cheating-fruit-are-taking-over-tiktok) (figures reported secondhand, partly from an embedded tweet).
- A May 2026 write-up describes a whole network of these accounts built on the same idea: recurring AI characters in absurd scenarios with a consistent style across episodes. Source: [Kilroy's Daily Briefings](https://kilroy-roboto-blog.pages.dev/2026-05-03-slop-network-recon.html).
- Storytime guides report that the most viral TikTok story series reach 10–50 million combined views across all their episodes. Source: [FluxNote storytime guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/storytime-tiktok-channel-ideas-2026) (tool vendor, unverified).

## Offer
- Free to watch; money comes later from creator funds, the YouTube Partner Program, brand deals and merchandise. No source in this run shows checked earnings for these accounts.
- Format: 30–90 second episodes, the same cast every time, a cliffhanger or twist at the end, numbered parts ("Part 7").

## Channels
- TikTok first (from source), with YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels as reposts (my guess).
- Growth comes from people following to see the next part (from source: the series format), plus comments arguing about the characters (my guess).

## My angle (three options)
1. **Same demand, different audience:** a cosy, kind series instead of cheating drama. For example, a cast of original "frequency spirits" (3, 6 and 9) who solve small everyday problems. It suits the 369 brand and families, and is safer for advertisers.
2. **Same demand, different channel:** run the series on YouTube Shorts, with every 10 episodes stitched into one longer YouTube video. Longer videos pay better and build a library.
3. **Same demand, one twist that Forge makes easy:** viewers vote in the comments on what happens next, and the next episode is made from the winning comment within 24 hours. Forge's storyboard, character lock and clover judge make fast, consistent episodes realistic.

## First step this week
Design two original characters in Forge Image mode (lock them with reference images and descriptions), then make 3 episodes of 30–45 seconds with the storyboard. Post one a day on TikTok and Shorts with the AI-content label on.
It worked if at least one episode gets clearly more views than your normal posts, and the comments ask for the next part.

## Why this could fail
- **Platform crackdown:** the Daily Dot headline says TikTok deleted some of these accounts. Low-effort AI "slop" is being pushed down, so the episodes need real stories and polish, not just shock value.
- **Copycat risk:** do not use fruit characters or copy any existing series' characters, names or plots. The format (recurring cast, cliffhangers) is free to use; the specific show is not.
- **Consistency is hard:** character consistency is the number one complaint about AI video (see the problems lane in `list.md`). Expect retries, and keep each shot simple.
- **The numbers are secondhand.** View counts come from news write-ups, not checked analytics, and viral peaks like these are rare.
