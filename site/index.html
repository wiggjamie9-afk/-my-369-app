The story

I made a vow at ten. It took me 38 years to keep it.

"I grew up with a dad whose love arrived as money, not presence. So I promised myself I'd be different. Late-diagnosed with ADHD and dyslexia, I lost my way, rebuilt my health, and learned the only thing that matters: being there. Not perfect. Present."

Dad's Code is everything I wish someone had handed me — for the dads still figuring it out.
