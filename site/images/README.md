# Site images

Drop your photo here as **jamie.jpg** (a warm shot of you + the kids works best).
As soon as `site/images/jamie.jpg` exists, it shows automatically in the Story section of index.html.

Ideal: portrait orientation, ~1000×1250px or larger, under ~1MB.
You can also use jamie.png — if so, change the `<img src>` in index.html to images/jamie.png.
