name: ask
description: Answer a question from the second brain with sources. Use when the user asks what they know about something, what was decided, what they promised, or anything about their business, projects or past notes.
---

Follow `brain/BRAIN.md`.

1. Read `brain/index.md` and `brain/overview.md` first, then only the pages they point to that are relevant (use Grep for names and keywords).
2. Easy questions (answer is on one or two pages): answer directly. Hard questions (needs four or more pages, a comparison or a new conclusion): use the `librarian` agent.
3. Cite every fact with a `[[link]]` to the page it came from. Say clearly what the brain does not know; never fill gaps with guesses.
4. If the answer is worth keeping, offer to save it as `brain/wiki/questions/short-question.md` with its sources, linked from `brain/index.md`.
