# The 48 AI Crew: sales playbook

Written by Claude, 2026-11-01, from research into how AI-receptionist and small-business SaaS companies sell. Numbers in this space are mostly vendor marketing — treat benchmarks as rough and measure your own. Follow `CLAUDE.md` rules; everything that contacts a person waits for approval and follows the Spam Act and Do Not Call rules.

## What the best do (grounded)

- Lead with **missed calls = lost jobs**. (Vendors claim up to 74% of small-business calls go unanswered — unverified, but it's the universal hook.)
- Price **low and comparable**: rivals start ~$29–30/month and compare to $550–1,500/month human answering services.
- Sell **emergency triage** hard to trades ("burst pipe → your mobile now").
- Use a **credit-card (opt-out) trial** — it converts far better than no-card trials.
- Win the trial in the **first 24–48 hours**; get them to a real booked call fast.
- Offer **done-for-you setup** (10 minutes) so the tradie never has to configure anything.

## The 48 AI Crew's unfair advantage: the demo

The thing that blows a tradie away is **hearing Jane answer as *their* business before they've paid a cent.** No competitor personalises the first touch. Your Demo Hunter (`docs/demo-hunter-brief.md`, `demo/index.html`) already does this: a page with their name, their missed-call cost, and a number where Jane answers "Dave's Plumbing". That is the wow moment. Build the whole funnel around it.

## The funnel

1. **Reach** (pick 1–2, go deep): local Facebook trade groups, a short daily TikTok/Short on "the $400 missed call", Google Business Profile + local SEO, trade-supplier and association partnerships, and personalised Demo Hunter outreach to tradies with weak after-hours cover.
2. **Hook:** the missed-call calculator → "you're losing ~$X/month".
3. **Wow:** "Call Jane now and hear her answer as your business."
4. **Trial:** 7 days, **card required** (opt-out), done-for-you setup in the first call.
5. **Activate (first 48h):** help them forward calls and set prices same-day; send "Jane answered X calls, booked Y jobs" on day 3.
6. **Convert:** day-6 "trial ends tomorrow, here's what Jane did + the bill link".
7. **Keep:** monthly "Jane booked you $X of jobs" report. This is the single biggest anti-cancellation tool.
8. **Grow:** referral offer ("refer a tradie mate, both get a month"), reviews, case studies.

## Pricing to test

- One simple plan to start, e.g. **$X/week, no lock-in, 7-day free trial** (fill in your real number; stay near or below rivals' ~$29–49/month to remove the price objection, or price higher if you include setup + emergency triage + monthly reports and say why).
- Later: a cheap tier (answering + texts) and a pro tier (bookings, calendar, emergency triage, monthly report, the [[Tradie knowledge base for Jane]]).

- The Command Center dashboard + approvals so you run it all from one screen.

## What NOT to do

- No bought lists, no cold SMS without consent, no bot spam — illegal (Spam Act) and it burns the brand.
- Don't invent testimonials, results or stats. Use only numbers Jane actually produced.
- Don't compete on price alone; compete on "never miss a job" + the personalised demo + emergency triage.

## The one metric that matters

**Cost to get one paying customer vs. what that customer pays you over their lifetime.** Track it from day one in the Command Center. Everything above is in service of making that ratio work.
