# Getting your first customers: the outreach kit

Written by Claude, 2026-11-02, for Jamie. This is the real "build" — the thing that turns a finished product into money. No mega-bot, no new tool. Just the messages that get Jane in front of tradies who'll pay.

**The rule (Australian law):** these go to people you have a reason to contact — tradies you know, mates of mates, warm referrals, businesses you've dealt with. Not scraped lists, not cold mass-texting. The Spam Act needs consent or an existing relationship, every message offers an easy "no worries, I'll leave it", and nothing auto-sends — you send each one by hand. (Mass cold outreach is the Demo Hunter brief's job, with its own suppression list and daily cap — not this.)

## Who to contact first (pick from people you can actually reach)

Best first customers are tradies who **miss calls and lose jobs because of it**:
- Solo or small operators (1–5 people) — no office, no receptionist.
- On the tools all day, phone in the ute or pocket.
- Trades with urgent call-outs: **plumbers, electricians, locksmiths, roof leaks, hot water, air-con, glaziers, tow/mechanical.**
- You already know them, or one degree away (your sparky, your mate's plumber, someone from a trade FB group you're in).

Make a list of **10 names** you could text without it being weird. That's the whole target.

## The messages — fill the [BLANKS], send one at a time

### 1. Text / WhatsApp to someone you know
> Hey [NAME], it's Jamie. Quick one — I've built an AI phone receptionist for tradies ("Jane") that answers your calls when you're on the tools, books the job and texts you the details, 24/7, in your business name. Reckon you miss many calls? I'm giving a few local [TRADE]s a free week to try it, no catch. Want me to set you up?

### 2. Follow-up if no reply (a few days later, once)
> No stress if it's not for you [NAME] — just thought of you 'cause you're flat out. The free week's there if you ever want to catch the calls you miss. Either way, hope works' good mate.

### 3. Referral ask (to a current/past contact)
> Hey [NAME], do you know any [plumbers/sparkies] who are flat out and miss calls? I've got an AI receptionist that catches their jobs for them and I'm giving a few a free week. Happy to shout you a [coffee/beer] if one signs up.

### 4. In-person / phone script (if you see them)
> "You know how you miss calls when you're under a house? I built a thing — an AI receptionist that answers as *your* business, books the job, texts you who rang and what they need. Costs nothing to try for a week. Want me to turn it on for you?"

## When one says yes

1. Point them at the demo page so they see it: `demo/index.html` personalised with their business (`?b=...&t=...&s=...`).
2. Set up the free 7-day trial (forward their calls, enter their prices/hours).
3. At the end of the week, send the **retention report** (`retention/index.html`) — "Jane booked you $X this week." That's what converts the trial to $299/month.
4. Ask the happy ones for one referral. Back to message #3.

## The honest targets

- **This week:** send 10 messages. Goal: 1 "yes, set me up."
- **That one customer** proves the whole system works and pays for itself.
- Everything else you've built — the organism, Forge, the command center — is waiting on this. One yes unlocks it.

## What not to do

- Don't buy a list or scrape numbers. Illegal here and it poisons your sender reputation.
- Don't mass-blast. Ten real, personal messages beat 1,000 cold ones.
- Don't wait for the product to be "perfect." It works now. The gap is customers, not code.

The next move is yours and it's tiny: pick one name off your list and send message #1 today.
