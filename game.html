HOW TO PLAY

  • 3
    Tap an empty square to place the next orb.
  • 6
    When 3 or more matching orbs touch (up, down, left or right), they merge into the next level where you placed the last one.
  • 9
    Merges can chain together for bonus points.
  • ✦
    Three stars together make a Supernova, which clears the squares around it.
  • A vortex orb removes any one orb you tap.
  • ⇄Tap Hold to save an orb for later, or swap it back.

The game ends when the board is full. Daily mode gives everyone the same orbs each day.
