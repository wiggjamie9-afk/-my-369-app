# Launch checklist

Everything that can be built is built. What's left is on your side, in this order.

## 1. Get it running (about 20 minutes)

1. Put both folders on your server, side by side: `quote-mate-hosted/` and `roof-quote-mate/`.
2. `cd quote-mate-hosted && bash deploy.sh`. It runs the tests, makes your owner password (shown once) and starts the app.
3. Point a domain at the server and add the Caddy lines the script prints. HTTPS matters: phones expect it, and
   sign-in cookies are only safe over it.
4. Run `PUBLIC_URL=https://your-domain bash deploy.sh` once more so emailed links use your real address.

## 2. Fill in your side

Open `/admin` and, under "Sales page details", set your service name, phone, email and the prices you want shown.
Until you do, the page says "Ask for pricing".

Optional but worth it: email for leads. Add `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASSWORD` and `SMTP_FROM`
to the env file (`/etc/quote-mate.env`, or `data/.env`) and run `deploy.sh` again. Without it, leads are still saved
in each tradie's inbox, but they won't be told by email.

## 2b. The newer features, and what each one needs

- **Embedding on a tradie's own website** needs nothing from you except HTTPS on your domain. They paste one line from
  Settings. Test it on one real customer's site (Wix, Squarespace and WordPress all accept it) before promising it to
  everyone, because I tested it on a plain page only.
- **The 30-day funnel** works as soon as people use the page.
- **Hot-lead flags, repeat detection and the "waiting more than a day" warning** work as soon as leads arrive.
- **Confirmation emails to customers, and lead emails to tradies, both need the SMTP settings.** Without them leads are
  still saved. With them, send a test lead to yourself first and check the emails don't land in spam: that depends on your
  mail provider and your domain, which I can't test from here.

## 3. Check before the first customer

- **Home batteries.** The preset's rebate is **$250 per kWh at the full rate**. I could not verify that figure: I confirmed the
  federal rebate's structure (full rate on the first 14 kWh, 60% to 28 kWh, 15% to 50 kWh, with a factor of 6.8 from May
  2026 that falls to 5.7 on 1 January 2027) but not what a certificate is worth in dollars. Each battery installer sets
  their own figure under Settings, and the page tells the customer it is an estimate. **Do not sell this to installers
  without checking the current figure with the Clean Energy Regulator or an installer, and tell them to re-check it on
  1 January 2027.**
- **Pet grooming.** The preset assumes the groomer does *not* charge GST (many small ones aren't registered). A groomer who
  is registered ticks one box in Settings and the page adds GST and says so.
- **Home care.** The rates are made-up examples. This is the most sensitive trade: someone asking about care for a relative
  is giving you personal information, even though the form asks for no health details and takes no photos. Get privacy
  advice before selling it, and have each provider confirm the wording about government funding (Support at Home) and
  how they're allowed to advertise. The price shown is a private-pay weekly estimate only.
- **Bookkeeping (Payday Check).** The seven-business-day rule on the page is from the ATO: super must be *received by
  the fund* within 7 business days of payday (20 for a new employee's or a new fund's first payment). The line about the
  free clearing house says only that the ATO told employers to move off it before July 2026, because I couldn't confirm
  the exact closure date. The page says it is general information, not advice. Check what a bookkeeper may say under
  their own registration (for example, tax and BAS agent rules) before they use it.
- **All trades.** Every preset's rates are made up examples. Each business's quote page says "example rates" until that
  business enters its own and confirms them.

## 4. Get customers

This is the part no code does. Start with the people you already know: roofers, painters, groomers and solar installers
you can ring or message today. Send them the demo link for their trade (the sales page lists all four), offer to set
them up, and add them under "Add a business". Aim for five paying customers before building anything else.

## 5. Before you charge anyone

- **Privacy and terms.** You'll hold homeowners' names, phone numbers, emails and photos on behalf of your customers.
  Get proper advice on a privacy policy and terms of service.
- **Payments.** There's no card handling. Invoice however you already do, then record "Paid until" in `/admin`. An
  overdue business gets a badge. Nothing switches off by itself.

## Backups

All business data is one file, `data/quotemate.db`, plus the photos in `data/photos/`. Copy the `data/` folder
somewhere safe on a schedule (a nightly copy to another machine is enough to start).


===== FILE: quote-mate-hosted/README.md =====
# Quote Mate, hosted

One small server that lets you sell the quote tool to many tradies as a monthly service.

- **Sales page** at `/` with a live demo for each of six trades (roofing, painting, pet grooming, home batteries, home care,
  bookkeeping) and an enquiry form.
- **Each business** gets its own quote page at `/q/<name>/`, priced from its own rates,
  and a lead inbox at `/q/<name>/admin` (leads, photos, status, notes, spreadsheet export,
  its own rates and wording).
- **You** get an owner console at `/admin`: add a business, set its plan, record what it
  has paid up to, pause it, reset its password, and read enquiries from the sales page.

Python 3 standard library only. No installs, no API keys in the repo. The quote page itself
is the tool in `../roof-quote-mate/`, served unchanged; this server supplies each business's
`config.js` and receives the leads.

## Run it

```bash
cd quote-mate-hosted
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests          # 34 tests
ADMIN_PASSWORD='pick-a-long-one' python3 server.py
# http://127.0.0.1:8090/        sales page
# http://127.0.0.1:8090/admin   owner console
```

To put it on the internet, run it behind Caddy or nginx with HTTPS and set `TRUST_PROXY=1`
and `PUBLIC_URL=https://your-domain`. Keep both folders (`quote-mate-hosted` and
`roof-quote-mate`) side by side, or point `QM_TOOL_DIR` at the quote tool.

## What makes it more than a calculator

- **Put it on your own website.** Settings gives each business one line of code. It adds the quote tool to any page, fits
  itself to the space, and only trusts messages from its own frame. Sign-in, inbox and settings pages can never be framed.
- **See that it works.** The inbox shows the last 30 days: opened, started, saw a price, sent details, each as a share of
  the step before, and what people ask about most. Counts are anonymous: no cookies, no addresses, nothing personal.
- **Qualify the lead.** Under the price the customer says how soon they want it and the best time to call. "As soon as
  possible" marks a **Hot** lead (and puts HOT in the email subject), the same phone number asking twice is flagged, and
  leads nobody has rung in a day are called out at the top of the inbox.
- **Confirm to the customer.** With email switched on, the homeowner gets an instant email with the ballpark they were
  shown and when to expect a call. A business can switch it off in Settings.
- **Brand colour.** A full-plan business can choose its own accent colour; it must be readable on the dark page, and the
  text colour on buttons is picked automatically.

Not built, and why: measuring a roof from satellite images (needs paid imagery and keys), text messages (needs an SMS
provider), and connections to specific CRMs beyond the webhook (Zapier and Make cover most of them).

## Branding

The service ships as **48 AI Crew Quoting** with the 48 AI Crew logo:

- The name is a setting: `/admin` > "Sales page details" > Service name. It shows on the sales page, in the browser tab and
  on every lite-plan quote page.
- The logo is two files in `static/`: `48-ai-crew-logo.png` (256 px, the sales page) and `48-ai-crew-icon.png` (96 px,
  headers, browser tab, home-screen icon). Replace both, keeping the file names, to rebrand.
- The colour is the logo's blue, `#3D8BFF` (text on it is `#0B1220`). It's set in two places that a test keeps in step:
  `static/site.css` (the sales page, inboxes and console) and `THEME` in `qm/store.py` (every quote page). An overdue payment
  is red, not blue, so it still reads as a warning.
- Plans: a **lite** business's quote page shows this brand. A **full** business's page shows only that business's own name
  and logo, never yours (its logo is uploaded under its Settings).

## Settings (environment variables)

| Variable | What it does |
|---|---|
| `ADMIN_PASSWORD` | Owner console password. The console is off without it |
| `HOST`, `PORT` | Where to listen. Default `127.0.0.1:8090` |
| `QM_DB` | SQLite file. Default `data/quotemate.db`; customer photos are stored beside it |
| `PUBLIC_URL` | Your public address, used in lead emails |
| `TRUST_PROXY=1` | Behind a reverse proxy: take the visitor's address from `X-Forwarded-For` |
| `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASSWORD`, `SMTP_FROM`, `SMTP_TLS` | Turns on lead emails. `SMTP_TLS` is `starttls` (default), `ssl` or `none` |

## How you'd use it

1. Open `/admin`, fill in "Sales page details": your service name, phone, and the prices
   you want shown. Until you set prices the page says "Ask for pricing".
2. When a tradie signs up, "Add a business". You're shown their sign-in page and a password
   **once**. Send both to them.
3. They sign in, put in their own rates, tick "These are my real rates", and share their link.
4. Leads land in their inbox, and in their email if you've set up SMTP.
5. When they pay, set "Paid until". An overdue business gets an "overdue" badge in your
   console. Nothing switches off by itself: pausing a business is your call.

## What it does not do

- **It does not take payments.** There is no Stripe or card handling. You invoice however
  you already do and record "Paid until" by hand.
- **No self sign-up.** A tradie sends an enquiry; you create their account.
- **No text messages.** Leads arrive in the inbox, by email (with SMTP set), and optionally
  as a copy posted to the tradie's own webhook (Zapier, Make).
- **Adding a new trade** means adding a file to `presets/` (copy `roofing.json`). A business
  can rename and re-price its options but not add or remove them from its settings page.
  Presets can also set `pricing.priceSuffix` ("per week"), `pricing.rebate`, a `watch` line on any option (listed
  under the price when picked), `words.privacyNote`, `photos.max: 0` (no photos) and `pricing.gstRegistered: false`.
- **Example rates.** The presets carry made-up rates. Each business's page says so until
  that business confirms its own.
- **The battery rebate figure is unverified.** See `LAUNCH.md` before selling to installers.
- **Privacy.** You will be holding homeowners' names, phones, emails and photos for your
  customers. Get proper advice on a privacy policy and terms before selling this.

## Safety built in

- A lead's price is worked out again on the server from the business's stored rates; a
  number sent by the browser is never trusted.
- Businesses are isolated: every lead and photo is looked up by business *and* id.
- Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes; a reset signs every device out.
- Uploads are checked by their actual bytes (PNG, JPG, WebP only; no SVG), size-capped,
  and stored under names the server chooses.
- Lead and enquiry forms are rate-limited and have a hidden field that traps bots.
- A business's webhook must be public HTTPS, so it can't be pointed at this server's own
  network.
- Spreadsheet exports defuse cells that would run as formulas.



===== FILE: quote-mate-hosted/deploy.sh =====
#!/bin/bash
# Quote Mate (hosted): one-command deploy on a Linux box.
#
#   bash deploy.sh
#   PORT=9000 PUBLIC_URL=https://quotes.example.com bash deploy.sh
#
# It checks Python, runs the tests, makes an owner password for this box (never kept in the repo),
# and starts the server: as a systemd service when run as root on a systemd machine, otherwise as a
# background process from this folder. Run it again any time to restart; your data is untouched.
# It never touches DNS, Caddy or any other service. The Caddy lines to add are printed at the end.
# The folder ../roof-quote-mate must sit next to this one (it is the quote page itself).
set -euo pipefail
cd "$(dirname "$0")"
HERE="$(pwd)"
PORT="${PORT:-8090}"

echo "== 1. Check =="
python3 -c 'import sys; assert sys.version_info >= (3, 8), "Python 3.8 or newer is needed"; print("python", sys.version.split()[0])'
[ -f ../roof-quote-mate/index.html ] || { echo "Can't find ../roof-quote-mate (the quote page). Put both folders side by side."; exit 1; }

echo "== 2. Tests =="
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests 2>&1 | tail -3

mkdir -p data
new_env() {   # $1 = file to create
  python3 - "$1" "$PORT" "${PUBLIC_URL:-}" <<'PY'
import os, secrets, sys
path, port, public = sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2], sys.argv[3]
fd = os.open(path, os.O_WRONLY | os.O_CREAT | os.O_EXCL, 0o600)
with os.fdopen(fd, "w") as fh:
    fh.write("ADMIN_PASSWORD=%s\nHOST=127.0.0.1\nPORT=%s\nTRUST_PROXY=1\n" % (secrets.token_urlsafe(14), port))
    if public:
        fh.write("PUBLIC_URL=%s\n" % public)
    fh.write("# To email leads to your customers, fill these in and run deploy.sh again:\n"
             "#SMTP_HOST=\n#SMTP_PORT=587\n#SMTP_USER=\n#SMTP_PASSWORD=\n#SMTP_FROM=\n")
PY
}

echo "== 3. Start =="
if [ "$(id -u)" = "0" ] && [ -d /run/systemd/system ]; then
  ENV_FILE=/etc/quote-mate.env
  [ -f "$ENV_FILE" ] || { new_env "$ENV_FILE"; FRESH=1; }
  OWNER="$(stat -c %U "$HERE")"
  cat > /etc/systemd/system/quote-mate.service <<UNIT
[Unit]
Description=Quote Mate (hosted)
After=network.target

[Service]
User=$OWNER
WorkingDirectory=$HERE
EnvironmentFile=$ENV_FILE
ExecStart=/usr/bin/env python3 $HERE/server.py
Restart=on-failure
NoNewPrivileges=true
PrivateTmp=true

[Install]
WantedBy=multi-user.target
UNIT
  systemctl daemon-reload
  systemctl enable quote-mate >/dev/null 2>&1
  systemctl restart quote-mate
  HOW="systemd service 'quote-mate'  (logs: journalctl -u quote-mate)"
else
  ENV_FILE="$HERE/data/.env"
  [ -f "$ENV_FILE" ] || { new_env "$ENV_FILE"; FRESH=1; }
  if [ -f data/server.pid ] && kill -0 "$(cat data/server.pid)" 2>/dev/null; then
    kill "$(cat data/server.pid)"; sleep 1          # only ever our own earlier process
  fi
  set -a; . "$ENV_FILE"; set +a
  nohup python3 "$HERE/server.py" > data/server.log 2>&1 &
  echo $! > data/server.pid
  HOW="background process $(cat data/server.pid)  (logs: $HERE/data/server.log, stop: kill \$(cat $HERE/data/server.pid))"
fi

PORT_NOW="$(grep '^PORT=' "$ENV_FILE" | cut -d= -f2)"
for _ in $(seq 1 20); do
  if python3 -c "import urllib.request; urllib.request.urlopen('http://127.0.0.1:$PORT_NOW/healthz', timeout=2)" 2>/dev/null; then UP=1; break; fi
  sleep 0.5
done
[ "${UP:-}" = "1" ] || { echo "It did not come up on port $PORT_NOW. Is something else using it? Check the logs."; exit 1; }

echo
echo "== UP =="
echo "Sales page:     http://127.0.0.1:$PORT_NOW/"
echo "Owner console:  http://127.0.0.1:$PORT_NOW/admin"
echo "Running as:     $HOW"
if [ "${FRESH:-}" = "1" ]; then
  echo "Owner password: $(grep '^ADMIN_PASSWORD=' "$ENV_FILE" | cut -d= -f2)   (shown once; kept in $ENV_FILE)"
else
  echo "Owner password: unchanged, in $ENV_FILE"
fi
echo
echo "To put it on the internet, add this to your Caddyfile yourself (this script does not touch Caddy or DNS):"
echo
echo "    quotes.example.com {"
echo "        reverse_proxy 127.0.0.1:$PORT_NOW"
echo "    }"
echo
echo "Then run:  PUBLIC_URL=https://quotes.example.com bash deploy.sh   so lead emails link to the right address."

"""Colour helpers: a business on the full plan can choose its own accent colour, and the pages must stay readable."""
import re

CHARCOAL = "#1B1D21"          # the page background the accent is read against
DARK_TEXT, LIGHT_TEXT = "#0B1220", "#FFFFFF"
HEX = re.compile(r"^#[0-9a-fA-F]{6}$")


def luminance(hex_colour):
    channels = [int(hex_colour[i:i + 2], 16) / 255 for i in (1, 3, 5)]
    lin = [c / 12.92 if c <= 0.03928 else ((c + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4 for c in channels]
    return 0.2126 * lin[0] + 0.7152 * lin[1] + 0.0722 * lin[2]


def contrast(a, b):
    hi, lo = sorted((luminance(a), luminance(b)), reverse=True)
    return (hi + 0.05) / (lo + 0.05)


def on_accent(accent):
    """Dark or white text, whichever reads better on a button of this colour."""
    return DARK_TEXT if contrast(DARK_TEXT, accent) >= contrast(LIGHT_TEXT, accent) else LIGHT_TEXT


def check_accent(value):
    """'' (use the crew blue) or a #RRGGBB colour that is readable as text on the dark page. Returns it normalised."""
    value = str(value or "").strip()
    if not value:
        return ""
    if not HEX.match(value):
        raise ValueError("the colour must look like #3D8BFF")
    value = value.upper()
    if contrast(value, CHARCOAL) < 4.5:
        raise ValueError("that colour is too dark to read on the dark background. Pick a lighter one")
    return value

def mail_settings(env):
    """SMTP details from the environment, or None when email is not set up. Nothing is stored in the repo."""
    if not env.get("SMTP_HOST") or not env.get("SMTP_FROM"):
        return None
    return {"host": env["SMTP_HOST"], "port": int(env.get("SMTP_PORT") or 587), "user": env.get("SMTP_USER", ""),
            "password": env.get("SMTP_PASSWORD", ""), "sender": env["SMTP_FROM"],
            "tls": env.get("SMTP_TLS", "starttls").lower()}          # starttls | ssl | none


def send_email(smtp, to, subject, body, reply_to=""):
    msg = EmailMessage()
    msg["From"], msg["To"], msg["Subject"] = smtp["sender"], to, subject
    if reply_to:
        msg["Reply-To"] = reply_to
    msg.set_content(body)
    if smtp["tls"] == "ssl":
        server = smtplib.SMTP_SSL(smtp["host"], smtp["port"], timeout=20, context=ssl.create_default_context())
    else:
        server = smtplib.SMTP(smtp["host"], smtp["port"], timeout=20)
    try:
        if smtp["tls"] == "starttls":
            server.starttls(context=ssl.create_default_context())
        if smtp["user"]:
            server.login(smtp["user"], smtp["password"])
        server.send_message(msg)
    finally:
        try:
            server.quit()
        except smtplib.SMTPException:
            pass

def safe_public_url(url, allow_private=False):
    """Only https, and only to a public address. Stops a webhook setting being used to reach
    this server's own network (a customer could otherwise point it at an internal service)."""
    parts = urllib.parse.urlsplit(url)
    if parts.scheme != "https" and not (allow_private and parts.scheme == "http"):
        raise ValueError("the webhook address must start with https://")
    if not parts.hostname or parts.username or parts.password:
        raise ValueError("that webhook address isn't valid")
    try:
        infos = socket.getaddrinfo(parts.hostname, parts.port or (443 if parts.scheme == "https" else 80), type=socket.SOCK_STREAM)
    except socket.gaierror:
        raise ValueError("that webhook address can't be found")
    for info in infos:
        ip = ipaddress.ip_address(info[4][0])
        if not allow_private and (ip.is_private or ip.is_loopback or ip.is_link_local or ip.is_reserved or ip.is_multicast or ip.is_unspecified):
            raise ValueError("the webhook address must be on the public internet")
    return url


class _NoRedirect(urllib.request.HTTPRedirectHandler):
    def redirect_request(self, *a, **k):
        return None                       # a redirect could point somewhere private: don't follow


def forward(url, payload, allow_private=False):
    safe_public_url(url, allow_private)
    req = urllib.request.Request(url, data=json.dumps(payload).encode(), headers={"Content-Type": "application/json",
                                                                                  "User-Agent": "QuoteMate/1"})
    with urllib.request.build_opener(_NoRedirect).open(req, timeout=15) as r:
        if not 200 <= r.status < 300:
            raise ValueError("webhook answered %d" % r.status)

"""The pricing sum and config check, server side. Mirrors roof-quote-mate/estimate.js so the price a
homeowner saw is the price recorded with their lead (the server works it out again; it never trusts
a number sent by the browser). tests/ checks the two agree on every combination."""
from decimal import Decimal, ROUND_HALF_UP, ROUND_FLOOR, ROUND_CEILING


def _num(v):
    return isinstance(v, (int, float)) and not isinstance(v, bool) and v == v and abs(v) != float("inf")


def check_config(c):
    """Everything wrong with a tenant config, in plain words. Empty list = usable."""
    bad = []
    if not isinstance(c, dict):
        return ["the configuration is missing"]
    for group, keys in (("jobTypes", ("labourPerUnit", "materialPerUnit")), ("difficulty", ("multiplier",)), ("sizes", ("min", "max"))):
        items = c.get(group)
        if not isinstance(items, list) or not items:
            bad.append("%s needs at least one entry" % group)
            continue
        seen = set()
        for i, item in enumerate(items, 1):
            where = "%s #%d" % (group, i)
            if not isinstance(item, dict) or not item.get("id") or not str(item.get("label") or "").strip():
                bad.append("%s needs a name" % where)
                continue
            where += " (%s)" % item["label"]
            if item["id"] in seen:
                bad.append("%s repeats an id" % where)
            seen.add(item["id"])
            for k in keys:
                if not _num(item.get(k)) or item[k] < 0:
                    bad.append("%s: %s must be a number, zero or more" % (where, k))
            if group == "sizes" and _num(item.get("min")) and _num(item.get("max")) and item["max"] < item["min"]:
                bad.append("%s: the largest size is smaller than the smallest" % where)
            if group == "difficulty" and item.get("multiplier") == 0:
                bad.append("%s: the multiplier can't be 0" % where)
            if item.get("watch") is not None and (not isinstance(item["watch"], str) or len(item["watch"]) > 300):
                bad.append("%s: the note must be text under 300 letters" % where)
    p = c.get("pricing") or {}
    if not _num(p.get("gstRate")) or not 0 <= p["gstRate"] <= 1:
        bad.append("GST rate should be like 0.10")
    if not _num(p.get("spread")) or not 0 <= p["spread"] < 1:
        bad.append("the range spread must be between 0% and 99%")
    if not _num(p.get("roundTo")) or p["roundTo"] < 1:
        bad.append("rounding must be $1 or more")
    if not _num(p.get("minimumJob")) or p["minimumJob"] < 0:
        bad.append("the minimum job must be zero or more")
    r = p.get("rebate")
    if r:
        if not _num(r.get("perUnit")) or r["perUnit"] < 0:
            bad.append("the rebate amount must be zero or more")
        bands, last = r.get("bands"), 0
        if not isinstance(bands, list) or not bands:
            bad.append("the rebate needs at least one band")
        else:
            for i, band in enumerate(bands, 1):
                if not isinstance(band, dict) or not _num(band.get("upTo")) or band["upTo"] <= last:
                    bad.append("rebate band %d: its limit must be bigger than the band before it" % i)
                else:
                    last = band["upTo"]
                if not isinstance(band, dict) or not _num(band.get("share")) or not 0 <= band["share"] <= 1:
                    bad.append("rebate band %d: its share must be between 0% and 100%%" % i)
    return bad


def _find(items, wanted):
    for item in items:
        if item["id"] == wanted:
            return item
    raise ValueError("unknown choice: %r" % (wanted,))


def _d(v):
    return Decimal(str(v))


def rebate_cents(c, units):
    """A size-based rebate (so many dollars off per unit, tapering by band). Cents; 0 when there is none."""
    r = c["pricing"].get("rebate")
    if not r:
        return Decimal(0)
    start, counted = Decimal(0), Decimal(0)
    for band in r["bands"]:
        counted += max(Decimal(0), min(_d(units), _d(band["upTo"])) - start) * _d(band["share"])
        start = _d(band["upTo"])
    return counted * (_d(r["perUnit"]) * 100).quantize(Decimal(1), ROUND_HALF_UP)


def price_note(c):
    """The words under a price: GST is only mentioned by a business that charges it."""
    bits = []
    if c["pricing"].get("priceSuffix"):
        bits.append(str(c["pricing"]["priceSuffix"]))
    if c["pricing"].get("gstRegistered") is not False:
        bits.append("including GST")
    if c["pricing"].get("rebate"):
        bits.append("after the rebate")
    return ", ".join(bits)


def estimate(c, type_id, difficulty_id, size_id):
    """-> dict(low, high, gross_low, gross_high, rebate_low, rebate_high, type, difficulty, size) in whole dollars.
    low/high is what the customer pays: GST included where it applies, any rebate taken off."""
    kind, diff, size = _find(c["jobTypes"], type_id), _find(c["difficulty"], difficulty_id), _find(c["sizes"], size_id)
    p = c["pricing"]
    whole = lambda v: v.quantize(Decimal(1), ROUND_HALF_UP)
    rate_cents = whole((_d(kind["labourPerUnit"]) + _d(kind["materialPerUnit"])) * 100)
    gst = Decimal(1) if (p.get("gstRegistered") is False or p.get("ratesIncludeGst")) else 1 + _d(p["gstRate"])
    step = _d(p["roundTo"])
    gross_low = _d(size["min"]) * rate_cents * _d(diff["multiplier"]) * (1 - _d(p["spread"])) * gst / 100
    gross_high = _d(size["max"]) * rate_cents * _d(diff["multiplier"]) * (1 + _d(p["spread"])) * gst / 100
    off_low, off_high = rebate_cents(c, size["min"]) / 100, rebate_cents(c, size["max"]) / 100
    down = lambda v: (whole(v) / step).to_integral_value(ROUND_FLOOR) * step
    up = lambda v: (whole(v) / step).to_integral_value(ROUND_CEILING) * step
    near = lambda v: (whole(v) / step).quantize(Decimal(1), ROUND_HALF_UP) * step
    low = max(down(gross_low - off_low), _d(p["minimumJob"]))
    high = max(up(gross_high - off_high), low)
    as_int = lambda v: int(v) if v == v.to_integral_value() else float(v)
    return {"low": as_int(low), "high": as_int(high), "gross_low": as_int(down(gross_low)), "gross_high": as_int(up(gross_high)),
            "rebate_low": as_int(near(off_low)), "rebate_high": as_int(near(off_high)), "type": kind, "difficulty": diff, "size": size}


def money(dollars):
    return "$" + format(int(round(dollars)), ",")

def now():
    return int(time.time())


def slugify(name):
    return re.sub(r"-{2,}", "-", re.sub(r"[^a-z0-9]+", "-", str(name).lower())).strip("-")[:40]


def hash_password(password, salt=None):
    salt = salt or secrets.token_bytes(16)
    return hashlib.scrypt(str(password).encode(), salt=salt, n=2 ** 14, r=8, p=1, dklen=32), salt


# columns added after the first release: older databases gain them on start-up, nothing else is touched
MIGRATIONS = (

class Store:
    def __init__(self, path, presets_dir):
        self.path, self.presets_dir = path, presets_dir
        os.makedirs(os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(path)) or ".", exist_ok=True)
        self.photo_dir = os.path.join(os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(path)), "photos")
        with self.tx() as c:
            c.executescript(SCHEMA)
            for table, column, ddl in MIGRATIONS:
                if column not in {r[1] for r in c.execute("PRAGMA table_info(%s)" % table)}:
                    c.execute("ALTER TABLE %s ADD COLUMN %s %s" % (table, column, ddl))
            c.execute("CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS leads_phone ON leads(tenant_id, phone_key)")

    @contextmanager
    def tx(self):
        con = sqlite3.connect(self.path, timeout=15)
        con.row_factory = sqlite3.Row
        con.execute("PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON")
        con.execute("PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL")
        try:
            yield con
            con.commit()
        except BaseException:
            con.rollback()
            raise
        finally:
            con.close()

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- site settings
    def site(self):
        out = dict(SITE_DEFAULTS)
        with self.tx() as c:
            for r in c.execute("SELECT key, value FROM settings"):
                if r["key"] in out:
                    out[r["key"]] = r["value"]
        return out

    def save_site(self, changes):
        with self.tx() as c:
            for k, v in changes.items():
                if k in SITE_DEFAULTS:
                    c.execute("INSERT INTO settings VALUES(?,?) ON CONFLICT(key) DO UPDATE SET value=excluded.value",
                              (k, " ".join(str(v).split())[:300]))
        return self.site()

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- presets
    def presets(self):
        return sorted(f[:-5] for f in os.listdir(self.presets_dir) if f.endswith(".json"))

    def preset(self, name):
        if name not in self.presets():
            raise ValueError("unknown trade preset: %s" % name)
        with open(os.path.join(self.presets_dir, name + ".json"), encoding="utf-8") as fh:
            return json.load(fh)

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- businesses
    def create_tenant(self, name, slug="", preset="roofing", tier="lite", contact_email="", phone="", demo=False):
        """-> (tenant, password). The password is returned once and stored only as a hash."""
        name = " ".join(str(name).split())[:80]
        if len(name) < 2:
            raise ValueError("the business needs a name")
        slug = slugify(slug or name)
        if not SLUG.match(slug) or slug in RESERVED:
            raise ValueError("the link name must be 3 to 40 letters, numbers or dashes (and not a reserved word)")
        if tier not in ("lite", "full"):
            raise ValueError("tier must be lite or full")
        config = self.preset(preset)
        password = secrets.token_urlsafe(9)
        pw_hash, salt = hash_password(password)
        with self.tx() as c:
            if c.execute("SELECT 1 FROM tenants WHERE slug=?", (slug,)).fetchone():
                raise ValueError("that link name is already taken: %s" % slug)
            c.execute("INSERT INTO tenants(slug,name,tier,demo,preset,config_json,rates_confirmed,phone,contact_email,pw_hash,pw_salt,created_at)"
                      " VALUES(?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?)",
                      (slug, name, tier, 1 if demo else 0, preset, json.dumps(config), 0, str(phone)[:40], str(contact_email)[:120],
                       pw_hash, salt, now()))
        return self.tenant(slug), password

    def tenant(self, slug=None, tenant_id=None):
        with self.tx() as c:
            r = c.execute("SELECT * FROM tenants WHERE " + ("slug=?" if slug is not None else "id=?"),
                          (slug if slug is not None else tenant_id,)).fetchone()
        if not r:
            return None
        t = dict(r)
        t["config"] = json.loads(t.pop("config_json"))
        return t

    def demos(self):
        """[(slug, product name)] for the public demos, one per trade, for the sales page."""
        with self.tx() as c:
            rows = c.execute("SELECT slug, config_json FROM tenants WHERE demo=1 AND active=1 ORDER BY slug").fetchall()
        trades = [json.loads(r["config_json"])["trade"] for r in rows]
        return [(r["slug"], t.get("label") or t["productName"]) for r, t in zip(rows, trades)]

    def tenants(self):
        since = now() - 30 * 86400
        with self.tx() as c:
            rows = c.execute(
                "SELECT t.id, t.slug, t.name, t.tier, t.active, t.demo, t.paid_until, t.preset, t.contact_email, t.phone,"
                " t.rates_confirmed, t.created_at,"
                " (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM leads l WHERE l.tenant_id=t.id) AS leads_total,"
                " (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM leads l WHERE l.tenant_id=t.id AND l.created_at>=?) AS leads_30d"
                " FROM tenants t ORDER BY t.demo, t.name COLLATE NOCASE", (since,)).fetchall()
        return [dict(r) for r in rows]

    def update_tenant(self, tenant_id, **changes):
        allowed = {"name", "tier", "active", "paid_until", "phone", "licence", "contact_email", "notify_email",
                   "forward_webhook", "rates_confirmed", "accent", "auto_reply"}
        sets, args = [], []
        for k, v in changes.items():
            if k not in allowed:
                raise ValueError("cannot change %s" % k)
            if k == "tier" and v not in ("lite", "full"):
                raise ValueError("tier must be lite or full")
            if k == "paid_until" and v and not re.match(r"^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$", str(v)):
                raise ValueError("paid until must be a date like 2027-01-31")
            if k == "name" and len(" ".join(str(v).split())) < 2:
                raise ValueError("the business needs a name")
            if k in ("notify_email", "contact_email") and v and not re.match(r"^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]{2,}$", str(v)):
                raise ValueError("that email address doesn't look right")
            if k == "forward_webhook" and v and not re.match(r"^https://[^\s]+$", str(v)):
                raise ValueError("the webhook address must start with https://")
            if k == "accent":
                v = check_accent(v)
            sets.append(k + "=?")
            args.append(int(bool(v)) if k in ("active", "rates_confirmed", "auto_reply") else " ".join(str(v).split())[:300])
        if sets:
            with self.tx() as c:
                c.execute("UPDATE tenants SET %s WHERE id=?" % ", ".join(sets), args + [tenant_id])
        return self.tenant(tenant_id=tenant_id)

    def save_config(self, tenant_id, config):
        problems = check_config(config)
        if problems:
            raise ValueError("; ".join(problems[:4]))
        with self.tx() as c:
            c.execute("UPDATE tenants SET config_json=? WHERE id=?", (json.dumps(config), tenant_id))

    def set_logo(self, tenant_id, data, ctype):
        with self.tx() as c:
            c.execute("UPDATE tenants SET logo=?, logo_type=? WHERE id=?", (data, ctype, tenant_id))

    def reset_password(self, tenant_id):
        password = secrets.token_urlsafe(9)
        pw_hash, salt = hash_password(password)
        with self.tx() as c:
            c.execute("UPDATE tenants SET pw_hash=?, pw_salt=? WHERE id=?", (pw_hash, salt, tenant_id))
        return password

    def check_password(self, tenant, password):
        return hmac.compare_digest(hash_password(password, tenant["pw_salt"])[0], tenant["pw_hash"])

    def public_config(self, tenant, lead_path):
        """The config.js contents for one business: its stored settings shaped the way the quote tool expects."""
        cfg = copy.deepcopy(tenant["config"])
        cfg.update({
            "tier": tenant["tier"],
            "business": {"name": tenant["name"], "logoPath": "logo" if tenant["logo"] else "", "phone": tenant["phone"], "licence": tenant["licence"]},
            "webhookUrl": "" if tenant["demo"] else lead_path,
            "ratesConfirmed": bool(tenant["rates_confirmed"]),
        })
        cfg["theme"] = dict(THEME)
        if tenant["tier"] == "full" and tenant["accent"]:          # a full plan can wear its own colour
            cfg["theme"] = {"accent": tenant["accent"], "onAccent": on_accent(tenant["accent"])}
        cfg["leadQuestions"] = cfg.get("leadQuestions") or copy.deepcopy(LEAD_QUESTIONS)
        if not tenant["demo"]:                                     # anonymous funnel counts; the public demos aren't counted
            cfg["eventsUrl"] = lead_path.rsplit("/", 1)[0] + "/event"
        if tenant["tier"] != "full":      # lite pages carry the product's brand; full pages carry the business's own
            cfg["brand"] = {"name": self.site()["service_name"], "logoPath": "/static/48-ai-crew-icon.png"}
        if not tenant["demo"]:            # a homeowner sees this until the business confirms its own rates
            cfg["noticeTitle"] = "Heads up"
            cfg["ratesNotice"] = "Example prices only. This business is still putting in its own rates, so treat these as a rough guide."
        if tenant["demo"]:
            cfg["demoNotice"] = "This is a demo with example prices. Nothing you enter here is sent anywhere."
        return cfg

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- leads
    def add_lead(self, tenant_id, lead, photos=()):
        key = re.sub(r"\D", "", lead["phone"])[-9:]                # the last nine digits: the same mobile written two ways matches
        with self.tx() as c:
            earlier = c.execute("SELECT id FROM leads WHERE tenant_id=? AND phone_key=? AND created_at>? ORDER BY id LIMIT 1",
                                (tenant_id, key, now() - 14 * 86400)).fetchone() if key else None
            cur = c.execute(
                "INSERT INTO leads(tenant_id,created_at,name,phone,email,suburb,job_type,difficulty,size,low,high,photos,"
                "urgency,contact_time,hot,phone_key,repeat_of) VALUES(?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?)",
                (tenant_id, now(), lead["name"], lead["phone"], lead["email"], lead["suburb"], lead["job_type"],
                 lead["difficulty"], lead["size"], lead["low"], lead["high"], len(photos),
                 lead.get("urgency", ""), lead.get("contact_time", ""), 1 if lead.get("hot") else 0, key,
                 earlier["id"] if earlier else 0))
            lead_id = cur.lastrowid
        if photos:
            folder = os.path.join(self.photo_dir, str(tenant_id), str(lead_id))
            os.makedirs(folder, exist_ok=True)
            for i, (data, ext) in enumerate(photos, 1):
                with open(os.path.join(folder, "%d.%s" % (i, ext)), "wb") as fh:
                    fh.write(data)
        return lead_id

    def photo(self, tenant_id, lead_id, n):
        """(bytes, content type) for one photo, only ever looked up under its own business's folder."""
        for ext, ctype in (("jpg", "image/jpeg"), ("png", "image/png"), ("webp", "image/webp")):
            path = os.path.join(self.photo_dir, str(int(tenant_id)), str(int(lead_id)), "%d.%s" % (int(n), ext))

                    return fh.read(), ctype
        return None

    def leads(self, tenant_id, status="", hot=False):

               + " ORDER BY created_at DESC, id DESC LIMIT 500")
        with self.tx() as c:
            return [dict(r) for r in c.execute(sql, (tenant_id, status) if status else (tenant_id,))]

        with self.tx() as c:
            return c.execute("SELECT COUNT(*) FROM leads WHERE tenant_id=? AND hot=1 AND status NOT IN ('won','lost')", (tenant_id,)).fetchone()[0]

    def update_lead(self, tenant_id, lead_id, status=None, notes=None, email_status=None, forward_status=None, reply_status=None):
        sets, args = [], []

                             (tenant_id, now() - hours * 3600)).fetchone()[0]

    def track(self, tenant_id, kind, option=None):
        """Count one step of the quote page. No cookies, no addresses, no personal data: a number per day."""
        kinds = [kind] + (["type:%s" % option] if kind == "price" and option else [])
        day = time.strftime("%Y-%m-%d")
        with self.tx() as c:
            for k in kinds:
                c.execute("INSERT INTO funnel VALUES(?,?,?,1) ON CONFLICT(tenant_id, day, kind) DO UPDATE SET n=n+1", (tenant_id, day, k))

    def funnel(self, tenant_id, days=30):
        """{'view','start','price','lead', 'types': {id: n}} for the last `days` days."""
        since = time.strftime("%Y-%m-%d", time.localtime(now() - days * 86400))
        out = {"view": 0, "start": 0, "price": 0, "types": {}}
        with self.tx() as c:
            for r in c.execute("SELECT kind, SUM(n) AS n FROM funnel WHERE tenant_id=? AND day>=? GROUP BY kind", (tenant_id, since)):

                    out["types"][r["kind"][5:]] = r["n"]
                else:
                    out[r["kind"]] = r["n"]
            out["lead"] = c.execute("SELECT COUNT(*) FROM leads WHERE tenant_id=? AND created_at>=?",
                                    (tenant_id, now() - days * 86400)).fetchone()[0]
            out["hot"] = c.execute("SELECT COUNT(*) FROM leads WHERE tenant_id=? AND hot=1 AND created_at>=?",
                                   (tenant_id, now() - days * 86400)).fetchone()[0]
        return out

def image_kind(data):
    """('jpg' | 'png' | 'webp', content type) from the file's first bytes, or None. The name and the
    type the browser claims are ignored; SVG is refused because it can carry script."""
    if data[:3] == b"\xff\xd8\xff":
        return "jpg", "image/jpeg"
    if data[:8] == b"\x89PNG\r\n\x1a\n":
        return "png", "image/png"
    if data[:4] == b"RIFF" and data[8:12] == b"WEBP":
        return "webp", "image/webp"
    return None

    # ---- signed session cookies
    def sign(self, scope):
        expiry = str(int(time.time()) + SESSION_DAYS * 86400)
        return expiry + "." + hmac.new(self.secret, (scope + "|" + expiry).encode(), hashlib.sha256).hexdigest()

    def signed(self, scope, token):
        expiry, _, sig = (token or "").partition(".")
        if not expiry.isdigit() or int(expiry) < time.time():
            return False
        return hmac.compare_digest(sig, hmac.new(self.secret, (scope + "|" + expiry).encode(), hashlib.sha256).hexdigest())

    @staticmethod
    def tenant_scope(tenant):                          # tied to the password, so a reset signs everyone out
        return "t%d:%s" % (tenant["id"], tenant["pw_hash"].hex()[:16])

    def allow(self, what, who, limit, window):
        """True if this is within `limit` tries per `window` seconds."""
        with self.lock:
            cutoff = time.time() - window
            recent = [t for t in self.hits.get((what, who), []) if t > cutoff]
            ok = len(recent) < limit
            if ok:
                recent.append(time.time())
            self.hits[(what, who)] = recent
            if len(self.hits) > 5000:
                self.hits = {k: v for k, v in self.hits.items() if v and v[-1] > cutoff}
            return ok

class Handler(BaseHTTPRequestHandler):
    app = None
    server_version = "QuoteMate/" + VERSION
    protocol_version = "HTTP/1.1"

    def log_message(self, fmt, *args):
        if self.app.env.get("QM_LOG"):
            sys.stderr.write("%s %s\n" % (time.strftime("%H:%M:%S"), fmt % args))

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- replies
    def send(self, status, body, ctype="text/html; charset=utf-8", headers=None, cache="no-store", embeddable=False):
        """embeddable: a public quote page, which a business may show inside its own website. Everything else refuses framing."""
        body = body if isinstance(body, (bytes, bytearray)) else str(body).encode()
        self.send_response(status)
        policy = CSP.replace("frame-ancestors 'self'", "frame-ancestors *") if embeddable else CSP
        for k, v in {"Content-Type": ctype, "Content-Length": str(len(body)), "Cache-Control": cache,
                     "X-Content-Type-Options": "nosniff", "Referrer-Policy": "same-origin", "Content-Security-Policy": policy,
                     **(headers or {})}.items():
            if isinstance(v, list):
                for item in v:
                    self.send_header(k, item)
            else:
                self.send_header(k, v)
        self.end_headers()
        if self.command != "HEAD":
            self.wfile.write(body)

    def redirect(self, to, headers=None):
        self.send(303, b"", headers={"Location": to, **(headers or {})})

    def json(self, status, data):
        self.send(status, json.dumps(data), "application/json; charset=utf-8")

    def cookie(self, name, value, path, age):
        secure = "; Secure" if self.headers.get("X-Forwarded-Proto") == "https" else ""
        return "%s=%s; Path=%s; Max-Age=%d; HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict%s" % (name, value, path, age, secure)

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- requests
    def cookies(self):
        out = {}
        for part in (self.headers.get("Cookie") or "").split(";"):
            k, _, v = part.strip().partition("=")
            out[k] = v
        return out

    def visitor(self):
        if self.app.trust_proxy and self.headers.get("X-Forwarded-For"):
            return self.headers["X-Forwarded-For"].split(",")[-1].strip()
        return self.client_address[0]

    def base_url(self):
        if self.app.env.get("PUBLIC_URL"):
            return self.app.env["PUBLIC_URL"].rstrip("/")
        scheme = "https" if self.headers.get("X-Forwarded-Proto") == "https" else "http"
        return "%s://%s" % (scheme, self.headers.get("Host", "localhost"))

    def same_origin(self):
        """Every form post must come from our own pages. Stops another site posting on a signed-in person's behalf."""
        origin = self.headers.get("Origin")
        if origin and urllib.parse.urlsplit(origin).netloc != self.headers.get("Host"):
            raise Halt(403, "That request came from another site.")

    def read(self, limit):
        try:
            length = int(self.headers.get("Content-Length") or 0)
        except ValueError:
            raise Halt(400, "Bad request.")
        if length > limit:
            self.close_connection = True
            raise Halt(413, "That is too large.")
        return self.rfile.read(length) if length else b""

    def form(self, limit=MAX_FORM):
        """(fields, files) from a urlencoded or multipart post. files: name -> (filename, bytes)."""
        raw = self.read(limit)
        ctype = self.headers.get("Content-Type", "")
        fields, files = {}, {}
        if ctype.startswith("multipart/form-data"):
            msg = BytesParser(policy=email_policy).parsebytes(b"Content-Type: " + ctype.encode("latin-1") + b"\r\n\r\n" + raw)
            if not msg.is_multipart():
                raise Halt(400, "Bad form.")
            for part in msg.iter_parts():
                name = part.get_param("name", header="content-disposition")
                if not name:
                    continue
                data = part.get_payload(decode=True) or b""
                if part.get_filename() is not None:
                    if data:
                        files[name] = (part.get_filename(), data)
                else:
                    fields[name] = data.decode("utf-8", "replace")
        else:
            fields = {k: v[0] for k, v in urllib.parse.parse_qs(raw.decode("utf-8", "replace"), keep_blank_values=True).items()}
        return fields, files

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- dispatch
    def do_GET(self):
        path = self.path.split("?", 1)[0]
        try:
            self.route(path)
        except Halt as h:
            if h.status in (301, 303):
                return self.send(h.status, b"", headers=h.headers)
            wants_json = path.endswith("/lead")
            body = json.dumps({"error": str(h)}) if wants_json else pages.layout("Sorry", "<h1>Sorry</h1><p class=\"lead\">%s</p>" % pages.e(str(h) or "That didn't work."))
            self.send(h.status, body, "application/json; charset=utf-8" if wants_json else "text/html; charset=utf-8", h.headers)
        except BrokenPipeError:
            pass
        except Exception as exc:
            sys.stderr.write("error on %s %s: %r\n" % (self.command, path, exc))
            self.send(500, pages.layout("Sorry", "<h1>Something went wrong</h1><p class=\"lead\">Nothing was lost. Please try again.</p>"))

    do_POST = do_HEAD = do_GET

    def query(self):
        return {k: v[0] for k, v in urllib.parse.parse_qs(self.path.partition("?")[2]).items()}

    def route(self, path):
        m, store = self.command, self.app.store
        if m == "POST":
            self.same_origin()

        if path == "/" and m in ("GET", "HEAD"):
            return self.send(200, pages.sales(store.site(), sent=self.query().get("sent") == "1", demos=store.demos()))
        if path == "/enquire" and m == "POST":
            return self.enquire()
        if path in STATIC_FILES and m in ("GET", "HEAD"):
            name, ctype, cache = STATIC_FILES[path]
            with open(os.path.join(ROOT, "static", name), "rb") as fh:
                return self.send(200, fh.read(), ctype, cache=cache)
        if path == "/healthz":
            return self.json(200, {"ok": True, "version": VERSION})
        if path == "/admin" or path.startswith("/admin/"):
            return self.owner(path)

        match = re.match(r"^/q/([a-z0-9-]{3,40})(/.*)?$", path)
        if match:
            tenant = store.tenant(match.group(1))
            if not tenant:
                raise Halt(404, "That page doesn't exist.")
            rest = match.group(2)
            if rest is None:
                raise Halt(301, headers={"Location": "/q/%s/" % tenant["slug"]})
            if rest == "/admin" or rest.startswith("/admin/"):
                return self.inbox(tenant, rest[len("/admin"):])
            return self.tool(tenant, rest)
        raise Halt(404, "That page doesn't exist.")

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- the public quote tool
    def tool(self, tenant, rest):
        m, slug = self.command, tenant["slug"]
        if rest == "/lead" and m == "POST":
            return self.lead(tenant)
        if rest == "/event" and m == "POST":
            return self.event(tenant)
        if m not in ("GET", "HEAD"):
            raise Halt(405, "Not allowed.")
        if rest == "/":
            if not tenant["active"]:
                return self.send(200, pages.paused(tenant), embeddable=True)
            with open(os.path.join(self.app.tool_dir, "index.html"), "rb") as fh:
                return self.send(200, fh.read(), cache="no-cache", embeddable=True)
        if rest == "/config.js":
            cfg = self.app.store.public_config(tenant, "/q/%s/lead" % slug)
            body = "window.RQM_CONFIG = %s;\n" % json.dumps(cfg, ensure_ascii=False).replace("</", "<\\/")
            return self.send(200, body, "text/javascript; charset=utf-8", cache="no-cache")
        if rest == "/full.js" and tenant["tier"] != "full":      # the plan is enforced here, not by a word in a file
            return self.send(200, "/* lite plan */\n", "text/javascript; charset=utf-8", cache="no-cache")
        if rest[1:] in TOOL_FILES:
            with open(os.path.join(self.app.tool_dir, rest[1:]), "rb") as fh:
                return self.send(200, fh.read(), TOOL_FILES[rest[1:]], cache="no-cache")
        if rest == "/logo":
            if tenant["logo"]:
                return self.send(200, bytes(tenant["logo"]), tenant["logo_type"], cache="no-cache")
            raise Halt(404, "No logo.")
        raise Halt(404, "That page doesn't exist.")

    def event(self, tenant):
        """One anonymous step of the quote page (opened, started, saw a price). Counted per day; nothing personal is kept."""
        app = self.app
        if tenant["active"] and not tenant["demo"] and app.allow("event", (tenant["id"], self.visitor()), 300, 3600):
            try:
                data = json.loads(self.read(2048) or b"{}")
            except ValueError:
                raise Halt(400, "Bad request.")
            kind = data.get("k") if isinstance(data, dict) else None
            if kind not in Store.FUNNEL_KINDS:
                raise Halt(400, "Bad request.")
            option = data.get("o") if kind == "price" else None
            if option not in {j["id"] for j in tenant["config"]["jobTypes"]}:
                option = None
            app.store.track(tenant["id"], kind, option)
        self.send(204, b"")

    def lead(self, tenant):
        app = self.app
        if not tenant["active"]:
            raise Halt(403, "Online quotes are switched off for this business.")
        if not app.allow("lead", (tenant["id"], self.visitor()), 8, 3600):
            raise Halt(429, "Too many requests from this connection. Please call us instead.")
        fields, files = self.form(MAX_LEAD)
        clean = lambda k, n: " ".join(fields.get(k, "").split())[:n]
        name, phone, email, suburb = clean("name", 80), clean("phone", 30), clean("email", 120), clean("suburb", 60)
        digits = re.sub(r"\D", "", phone)
        if len(name) < 2 or len(suburb) < 2 or not 8 <= len(digits) <= 12 or re.search(r"[^\d\s()+\-.]", phone) or not EMAIL.match(email):
            raise Halt(400, "Some details are missing or don't look right.")
        try:
            est = estimate(tenant["config"], fields.get("type_id"), fields.get("difficulty_id"), fields.get("size_id"))
        except (ValueError, KeyError):
            raise Halt(400, "That choice isn't one of the options.")
        if fields.get("company") or tenant["demo"]:               # a bot, or the public demo: accept and drop
            return self.json(200, {"ok": True})

        photos = []
        if tenant["tier"] == "full":
            for key in sorted(k for k in files if re.match(r"^photo\d$", k))[: int(tenant["config"]["photos"]["max"])]:
                data = files[key][1]
                kind = image_kind(data)
                if kind and len(data) <= MAX_PHOTO:
                    photos.append((data, kind[0]))
        questions = tenant["config"].get("leadQuestions") or LEAD_QUESTIONS
        pick = lambda key, field: next((o for o in (questions.get(key) or {}).get("options", []) if o["id"] == fields.get(field)), None)
        urgency, when = pick("urgency", "urgency"), pick("contactTime", "contact_time")
        lead = {"name": name, "phone": phone, "email": email, "suburb": suburb, "job_type": est["type"]["label"],
                "difficulty": est["difficulty"]["label"], "size": est["size"]["label"], "low": est["low"], "high": est["high"],

    def save_settings(self, tenant):
        app, store = self.app, self.app.store
        fields, files = self.form(MAX_LOGO + MAX_FORM)
        cfg = json.loads(json.dumps(tenant["config"]))

        def number(key, label):
            try:
                v = float(fields.get(key, "").strip())
            except ValueError:
                raise ValueError("%s must be a number" % label)
            if v != v or v in (float("inf"), float("-inf")) or v < 0:
                raise ValueError("%s must be zero or more" % label)
            return int(v) if v == int(v) else v
        text = lambda key, old, n=60: " ".join(fields.get(key, old).split())[:n] or old
        try:
            for j in cfg["jobTypes"]:
                j["label"] = text("jt_%s_label" % j["id"], j["label"])
                j["labourPerUnit"] = number("jt_%s_labour" % j["id"], j["label"] + " labour")
                j["materialPerUnit"] = number("jt_%s_material" % j["id"], j["label"] + " materials")
            for d in cfg["difficulty"]:
                d["label"] = text("df_%s_label" % d["id"], d["label"])
                d["multiplier"] = number("df_%s_mult" % d["id"], d["label"] + " multiplier")
            for s in cfg["sizes"]:
                s["label"] = text("sz_%s_label" % s["id"], s["label"])
                s["min"], s["max"] = number("sz_%s_min" % s["id"], s["label"] + " from"), number("sz_%s_max" % s["id"], s["label"] + " to")
            p = cfg["pricing"]
            p["gstRegistered"] = fields.get("gst_registered") == "1"
            p["ratesIncludeGst"] = fields.get("gst_included") == "1"
            if p.get("rebate"):
                p["rebate"]["perUnit"] = number("rebate_per_unit", "The rebate amount")
            p["spread"] = number("spread_pct", "The range spread") / 100
            p["roundTo"], p["minimumJob"] = number("round_to", "Rounding"), number("minimum_job", "The smallest job")
            for key, n in (("headline", 90), ("subline", 140), ("ballparkNote", 200), ("thanksSub", 140)):
                cfg["words"][key] = text("w_" + key, cfg["words"][key], n)
            if fields.get("forward_webhook", "").strip():
                deliver.safe_public_url(fields["forward_webhook"].strip(), app.allow_private_forward)
            logo = files.get("logo")
            if logo:
                kind = image_kind(logo[1])
                if not kind or len(logo[1]) > MAX_LOGO:
                    raise ValueError("The logo must be a PNG or JPG under 400 KB")
            accent = check_accent(fields.get("accent", "")) if tenant["tier"] == "full" else ""      # checked before anything is saved
            store.save_config(tenant["id"], cfg)                       # runs the full config check
            store.update_tenant(tenant["id"], name=fields.get("name", tenant["name"]), phone=fields.get("phone", ""),
                                licence=fields.get("licence", ""), notify_email=fields.get("notify_email", "").strip(),

def _quietly(fn, *args):
    try:
        fn(*args)
    except Exception as exc:
        sys.stderr.write("background task failed: %r\n" % (exc,))


def make_server(env):
    host, port = env.get("HOST", "127.0.0.1"), int(env.get("PORT") or 8090)
    handler = type("BoundHandler", (Handler,), {"app": App(env)})
    server = ThreadingHTTPServer((host, port), handler)
    server.daemon_threads = True
    return server


def main():
    server = make_server(dict(os.environ))
    app = server.RequestHandlerClass.app
    print("Quote Mate (hosted) %s on http://%s:%d/" % (VERSION, server.server_address[0], server.server_address[1]))
    print("  owner console: %s" % ("/admin" if app.admin_password else "OFF - set ADMIN_PASSWORD to use it"))
    print("  lead emails:   %s" % ("on, via " + app.smtp["host"] if app.smtp else "off - set SMTP_HOST and SMTP_FROM to turn on"))
    print("  demo:          /q/demo/", flush=True)
    try:
        server.serve_forever()
    except KeyboardInterrupt:
        print()
    finally:
        server.server_close()


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

/* 48 AI Crew Quoting: put a business's quote page on its own website.
   <script src="https://your-domain/embed.js" data-business="your-link-name" async></script>
   It adds an iframe where the script tag is, and grows it to fit. It reads nothing from your page. */
(function () {
  "use strict";
  var script = document.currentScript;
  if (!script || !script.src) return;
  var slug = script.getAttribute("data-business");
  if (!slug || !/^[a-z0-9-]{3,40}$/.test(slug)) return;
  var origin = new URL(script.src).origin;

  var frame = document.createElement("iframe");
  frame.src = origin + "/q/" + slug + "/?embed=1";
  frame.title = script.getAttribute("data-title") || "Get an instant price";
  frame.setAttribute("scrolling", "no");
  frame.setAttribute("loading", "lazy");
  frame.style.cssText = "display:block;width:100%;max-width:640px;margin:0 auto;border:0;border-radius:16px;min-height:520px;color-scheme:dark";
  script.parentNode.insertBefore(frame, script.nextSibling);

  window.addEventListener("message", function (e) {
    // only listen to our own frame, on our own origin
    if (e.origin !== origin || e.source !== frame.contentWindow || !e.data || typeof e.data !== "object") return;
    if (e.data.qm === "height" && typeof e.data.h === "number" && e.data.h > 100 && e.data.h < 20000) {
      frame.style.height = Math.ceil(e.data.h) + "px";
    } else if (e.data.qm === "top") {                         // a new step opened: bring the top of the widget into view
      var top = frame.getBoundingClientRect().top;
      if (top < 0) window.scrollTo({ top: window.pageYOffset + top - 8, behavior: "smooth" });
    }
  });
})();

class NoRedirect(urllib.request.HTTPRedirectHandler):
    def redirect_request(self, *a, **k):
        return None


class Client:
    """A tiny browser: keeps cookies per path, sends forms, never follows redirects on its own."""

    def __init__(self, base):
        self.base, self.jar = base, {}
        self.opener = urllib.request.build_opener(NoRedirect)

    def call(self, method, path, form=None, files=None, headers=None, raw=None):
        data, hdrs = None, dict(headers or {})
        if raw is not None:
            data, hdrs["Content-Type"] = raw, "application/json"
        elif files is not None:
            boundary = "----test%d" % time.time_ns()
            parts = []
            for k, v in (form or {}).items():
                parts.append(('--%s\r\nContent-Disposition: form-data; name="%s"\r\n\r\n%s\r\n' % (boundary, k, v)).encode())
            for k, (fname, blob) in files.items():
                parts.append(('--%s\r\nContent-Disposition: form-data; name="%s"; filename="%s"\r\nContent-Type: application/octet-stream\r\n\r\n'
                              % (boundary, k, fname)).encode() + blob + b"\r\n")
            data = b"".join(parts) + ("--%s--\r\n" % boundary).encode()
            hdrs["Content-Type"] = "multipart/form-data; boundary=" + boundary
        elif form is not None:
            data = urllib.parse.urlencode(form).encode()
            hdrs["Content-Type"] = "application/x-www-form-urlencoded"
        cookies = "; ".join("%s=%s" % (n, v) for (n, p), v in self.jar.items() if path.startswith(p))
        if cookies:
            hdrs["Cookie"] = cookies
        req = urllib.request.Request(self.base + path, data=data, method=method, headers=hdrs)
        try:
            with self.opener.open(req, timeout=10) as r:
                status, h, body = r.status, r.headers, r.read()
        except urllib.error.HTTPError as e:
            status, h, body = e.code, e.headers, e.read()
        for raw in h.get_all("Set-Cookie") or []:
            name, _, value = raw.split(";")[0].partition("=")
            self.jar[(name, re.search(r"Path=([^;]+)", raw).group(1))] = value
        return status, body, h

    def text(self, method, path, **kw):
        status, body, h = self.call(method, path, **kw)
        return status, body.decode("utf-8", "replace")

def luminance(hex_colour):
    channels = [int(hex_colour[i:i + 2], 16) / 255 for i in (1, 3, 5)]
    lin = [c / 12.92 if c <= 0.03928 else ((c + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4 for c in channels]
    return 0.2126 * lin[0] + 0.7152 * lin[1] + 0.0722 * lin[2]


def contrast(a, b):
    hi, lo = sorted((luminance(a), luminance(b)), reverse=True)
    return (hi + 0.05) / (lo + 0.05)

class Funnel(Base):
    """Proof the quote page is working: anonymous counts, no cookies, nothing personal."""

    def event(self, slug, kind, option="", headers=None):
        return self.c.call("POST", "/q/%s/event" % slug, raw=json.dumps({"k": kind, "o": option}).encode(), headers=headers)

    def test_steps_are_counted_and_shown_against_the_step_before(self):
        t, pw = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        for _ in range(10):
            self.assertEqual(self.event("acme-roofing", "view")[0], 204)
        for _ in range(5):
            self.event("acme-roofing", "start")
        for kind_option in ("tile", "tile", "tin", "colorbond"):
            self.event("acme-roofing", "price", kind_option)
        self.lead("acme-roofing")
        f = self.store.funnel(t["id"])
        self.assertEqual((f["view"], f["start"], f["price"], f["lead"]), (10, 5, 4, 1))
        self.assertEqual(f["types"], {"tile": 2, "tin": 1, "colorbond": 1})
        page = self.signed_in(t, pw).text("GET", "/q/acme-roofing/admin")[1]
        for expected in ("Opened your quote page", "50% of the step before", "80% of the step before", "25% of the step before",
                         "Most asked about: Tile (2)", "Anonymous counts only. No cookies"):
            self.assertIn(expected, page)

    def test_nothing_personal_is_kept_and_no_cookie_is_set(self):
        t, _ = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        status, body, headers = self.event("acme-roofing", "view")
        self.assertEqual((status, body, headers.get("Set-Cookie")), (204, b"", None))
        with self.store.tx() as db:
            self.assertEqual([r[1] for r in db.execute("PRAGMA table_info(funnel)")], ["tenant_id", "day", "kind", "n"])   # a number per day, no addresses

    def test_junk_and_abuse_are_refused_or_ignored(self):
        t, _ = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        self.assertEqual(self.event("acme-roofing", "purchase")[0], 400)
        self.assertEqual(self.c.call("POST", "/q/acme-roofing/event", raw=b"{nope")[0], 400)
        self.assertEqual(self.c.call("POST", "/q/acme-roofing/event", raw=b"[1,2]")[0], 400)
        self.event("acme-roofing", "price", "../../etc/passwd")                           # an option that isn't one of theirs is dropped
        self.assertEqual(self.store.funnel(t["id"])["types"], {})
        self.assertEqual(self.store.funnel(t["id"])["price"], 1)                          # ...but the step itself still counts
        self.assertEqual(self.event("acme-roofing", "view", headers={"Origin": "https://evil.example"})[0], 403)
        self.store.update_tenant(t["id"], active=False)
        self.assertEqual(self.event("acme-roofing", "view")[0], 204)
        self.assertEqual(self.store.funnel(t["id"])["view"], 0)                          # a paused page counts nothing
        self.assertEqual(self.event("demo", "view")[0], 204)
        self.assertEqual(self.store.funnel(self.store.tenant("demo")["id"])["view"], 0)   # nor does a demo
        self.assertEqual(self.c.call("POST", "/q/nobody-here/event", raw=b"{}")[0], 404)

    def test_counting_is_rate_limited_without_telling_a_customer(self):
        t, _ = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        codes = {self.event("acme-roofing", "view")[0] for _ in range(310)}
        self.assertEqual(codes, {204})                                                    # a flood never errors for the visitor...
        self.assertEqual(self.store.funnel(t["id"])["view"], 300)                         # ...but only the first 300 an hour are counted

    def test_an_empty_funnel_says_how_to_get_visitors(self):
        t, pw = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        self.assertIn("No visits yet", self.signed_in(t, pw).text("GET", "/q/acme-roofing/admin")[1])


class Embedding(Base):
    """The quote tool on a business's own website."""

    def test_the_embed_script_is_served_and_only_trusts_its_own_frame(self):
        status, body, h = self.c.call("GET", "/embed.js")
        js = body.decode()
        self.assertEqual((status, h["Content-Type"]), (200, "text/javascript; charset=utf-8"))
        for expected in ('data-business', '/^[a-z0-9-]{3,40}$/', "e.origin !== origin", "e.source !== frame.contentWindow", "?embed=1"):
            self.assertIn(expected, js)

    def test_quote_pages_may_be_framed_anywhere_but_nothing_else_may(self):
        t, pw = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        quote = self.c.call("GET", "/q/acme-roofing/?embed=1")[2]["Content-Security-Policy"]
        self.assertIn("frame-ancestors *", quote)
        self.store.update_tenant(t["id"], active=False)
        self.assertIn("frame-ancestors *", self.c.call("GET", "/q/acme-roofing/")[2]["Content-Security-Policy"])    # the paused notice too
        for path in ("/", "/admin", "/q/acme-roofing/admin", "/q/acme-roofing/config.js", "/static/site.css"):
            policy = self.c.call("GET", path)[2]["Content-Security-Policy"]
            self.assertIn("frame-ancestors 'self'", policy, path)                         # sign-in and inbox pages can never be framed
            self.assertNotIn("frame-ancestors *", policy, path)

    def test_the_settings_page_hands_over_the_embed_code(self):
        t, pw = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        page = self.signed_in(t, pw).text("GET", "/q/acme-roofing/admin/settings")[1]
        self.assertIn("&lt;script src=&quot;%s/embed.js&quot; data-business=&quot;acme-roofing&quot; async&gt;&lt;/script&gt;" % self.base, page)
        self.assertIn("Put it on your website", page)


class Colour(Base):
    def test_a_full_plan_can_wear_its_own_colour_and_stays_readable(self):
        from qm.colour import contrast, on_accent
        t, pw = self.business("Top Roofs", tier="full")
        c = self.signed_in(t, pw)
        form = Settings.form_for(self, t, accent="#e4572e")
        self.assertEqual(c.call("POST", "/q/top-roofs/admin/settings", form=form, files={})[0], 303)
        js = self.c.text("GET", "/q/top-roofs/config.js")[1]
        cfg = json.loads(js[js.index("{"):js.rindex("}") + 1])
        self.assertEqual(cfg["theme"]["accent"], "#E4572E")
        self.assertEqual(cfg["theme"]["onAccent"], on_accent("#E4572E"))
        self.assertGreaterEqual(contrast(cfg["theme"]["onAccent"], cfg["theme"]["accent"]), 4.5)
        before = self.store.tenant("top-roofs")["config"]
        for bad, why in (("blue", "must look like"), ("#123456", "too dark"), ("#1B1D21", "too dark"), ("#GGGGGG", "must look like")):
            status, page = c.text("POST", "/q/top-roofs/admin/settings", form=Settings.form_for(self, t, accent=bad, jt_tile_labour="99"), files={})
            self.assertEqual(status, 400, bad)
            self.assertIn(why, page)
        self.assertEqual(self.store.tenant("top-roofs")["config"], before)                # a refused colour saves nothing, rates included
        self.assertEqual(self.store.tenant("top-roofs")["accent"], "#E4572E")
        form = Settings.form_for(self, t, accent="")
        c.call("POST", "/q/top-roofs/admin/settings", form=form, files={})
        js = self.c.text("GET", "/q/top-roofs/config.js")[1]
        self.assertIn('"accent": "#3D8BFF"', js)                                          # empty goes back to the crew blue

    def test_a_lite_plan_cannot_change_the_colour(self):
        t, pw = self.business("Acme Roofing", tier="lite")
        c = self.signed_in(t, pw)
        self.assertNotIn('name="accent"', c.text("GET", "/q/acme-roofing/admin/settings")[1])
        c.call("POST", "/q/acme-roofing/admin/settings", form=Settings.form_for(self, t, accent="#E4572E"), files={})
        js = self.c.text("GET", "/q/acme-roofing/config.js")[1]
        self.assertIn('"accent": "#3D8BFF"', js)

class FakeSmtp(socketserver.StreamRequestHandler):
    """Speaks just enough SMTP to receive one message."""
    inbox = []

    def handle(self):
        self.wfile.write(b"220 test ESMTP\r\n")
        data, in_data = [], False
        for raw in self.rfile:
            line = raw.decode("utf-8", "replace")
            if in_data:
                if line == ".\r\n":
                    FakeSmtp.inbox.append("".join(data))
                    in_data = False
                    self.wfile.write(b"250 queued\r\n")
                else:
                    data.append(line)
            elif line[:4].upper() == "DATA":
                in_data = True
                self.wfile.write(b"354 go on\r\n")
            elif line[:4].upper() == "QUIT":
                self.wfile.write(b"221 bye\r\n")
                return
            else:
                self.wfile.write(b"250 ok\r\n")


class Hook(BaseHTTPRequestHandler):
    got = []

    def do_POST(self):
        Hook.got.append(json.loads(self.rfile.read(int(self.headers["Content-Length"]))))
        self.send_response(200)
        self.send_header("Content-Length", "0")
        self.end_headers()

    def log_message(self, *a):
        pass


class Delivery(Base):
    def setUp(self):
        FakeSmtp.inbox, Hook.got = [], []
        self.smtp = socketserver.ThreadingTCPServer(("127.0.0.1", 0), FakeSmtp)
        self.hook = HTTPServer(("127.0.0.1", 0), Hook)
        for s in (self.smtp, self.hook):
            threading.Thread(target=s.serve_forever, daemon=True).start()
        self.ENV = {"SMTP_HOST": "127.0.0.1", "SMTP_PORT": str(self.smtp.server_address[1]), "SMTP_FROM": "leads@quotemate.example",
                    "SMTP_TLS": "none", "QM_ALLOW_PRIVATE_FORWARD": "1", "PUBLIC_URL": "https://quotes.example.com"}
        super().setUp()

    def tearDown(self):
        super().tearDown()
        for s in (self.smtp, self.hook):
            s.shutdown()
            s.server_close()

    def test_a_new_lead_is_emailed_and_copied_to_the_tradies_webhook(self):
        t, _ = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        self.store.update_tenant(t["id"], notify_email="boss@acme.example")
        with self.store.tx() as c:                                    # test-only: a local address, normally refused by the settings page
            c.execute("UPDATE tenants SET forward_webhook=? WHERE id=?", ("http://127.0.0.1:%d/hook" % self.hook.server_address[1], t["id"]))
        self.assertEqual(self.lead("acme-roofing", name="Sam\r\nBcc: everyone@evil.example")[0], 200)
        lead = self.wait_for(lambda: next((l for l in self.store.leads(t["id"]) if l["email_status"] and l["forward_status"]), None))
        self.assertEqual((lead["email_status"], lead["forward_status"]), ("sent", "sent"))
        mail = FakeSmtp.inbox[0]
        for expected in ("To: boss@acme.example", "From: leads@quotemate.example", "Reply-To: sam@example.com", "0400 000 000",
                         "$17,800 to $36,300 including GST", "https://quotes.example.com/q/acme-roofing/admin/lead/%d" % lead["id"]):
            self.assertIn(expected, mail)
        headers = mail.split("\r\n\r\n")[0]
        self.assertFalse([h for h in headers.split("\r\n") if h.lower().startswith("bcc")])   # a name can't smuggle in a mail header
        self.assertIn("Subject: New quote request: Sam Bcc: everyone@evil.example, Fremantle", headers)   # it stays inside the subject text
        self.assertEqual((Hook.got[0]["name"], Hook.got[0]["low"], Hook.got[0]["business"]), ("Sam Bcc: everyone@evil.example", 17800, "Acme Roofing"))

    def test_a_dead_mail_server_never_loses_the_lead(self):
        t, _ = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        self.store.update_tenant(t["id"], notify_email="boss@acme.example")
        self.app.smtp["port"] = 1                                      # nothing is listening there
        self.assertEqual(self.lead("acme-roofing")[0], 200)
        lead = self.wait_for(lambda: next((l for l in self.store.leads(t["id"]) if l["email_status"]), None))
        self.assertTrue(lead["email_status"].startswith("failed"))
        self.assertEqual(lead["name"], "Sam Sample")


    def test_the_homeowner_gets_a_confirmation_and_can_be_switched_off(self):
        t, _ = self.business("Acme Roofing", phone="08 1111 1111")
        self.store.update_tenant(t["id"], notify_email="boss@acme.example")
        self.assertEqual(self.lead("acme-roofing", name="Sam\r\nBcc: everyone@evil.example", contact_time="morning", urgency="asap")[0], 200)
        lead = self.wait_for(lambda: next((l for l in self.store.leads(t["id"]) if l["reply_status"]), None))
        self.assertEqual(lead["reply_status"], "sent")
        self.wait_for(lambda: len(FakeSmtp.inbox) >= 2)
        mails = {("sam@example.com" if "\r\nTo: sam@example.com\r\n" in m else "boss@acme.example"): m for m in FakeSmtp.inbox}
        reply = mails["sam@example.com"]
        for expected in ("Subject: Your quote request to Acme Roofing", "Hi Sam,", "$17,800 to $36,300 including GST",
                         "best time to call is: morning", "Call 08 1111 1111", "Reply-To: boss@acme.example"):
            self.assertIn(expected, reply)
        self.assertFalse([h for h in reply.split("\r\n\r\n")[0].split("\r\n") if h.lower().startswith("bcc")])
        boss = mails["boss@acme.example"]
        self.assertIn("Subject: HOT: New quote request", boss)                              # the tradie's email leads with the hot flag
        self.assertIn("Wants it:  As soon as possible", boss)

        FakeSmtp.inbox.clear()
        self.store.update_tenant(t["id"], auto_reply=False)
        self.lead("acme-roofing", name="Second Person", phone="0433 444 555")
        self.wait_for(lambda: len(FakeSmtp.inbox) >= 1)
        time.sleep(0.4)
        self.assertEqual(len(FakeSmtp.inbox), 1)                                            # only the tradie's email
        self.assertIn("To: boss@acme.example", FakeSmtp.inbox[0])

    def test_no_mail_server_means_no_confirmation_but_the_lead_is_safe(self):
        t, _ = self.business("Acme Roofing")
        self.app.smtp = None
        self.lead("acme-roofing")
        lead = self.wait_for(lambda: next((l for l in self.store.leads(t["id"]) if l["reply_status"]), None))
        self.assertEqual(lead["reply_status"], "not sent: email is not set up")
        self.assertEqual(lead["name"], "Sam Sample")

        self.assertEqual(server.image_kind(JPEG), ("jpg", "image/jpeg"))
        self.assertEqual(server.image_kind(PNG), ("png", "image/png"))
        self.assertEqual(server.image_kind(b"RIFF\x00\x00\x00\x00WEBPVP8 "), ("webp", "image/webp"))

            self.assertIsNone(server.image_kind(bad))


class PricingAgreesWithTheBrowser(unittest.TestCase):
    """The server's sum and the quote tool's sum must give the same range for every choice."""

    def test_presets_are_valid(self):
        for name in ("roofing", "painting", "pets", "batteries", "homecare", "bookkeeping"):
            with open(os.path.join(ROOT, "presets", name + ".json"), encoding="utf-8") as fh:
                self.assertEqual(check_config(json.load(fh)), [], name)

    @unittest.skipUnless(shutil.which("node"), "node is not installed")
    def test_every_combination_matches_estimate_js(self):
        tool = os.path.join(os.path.dirname(ROOT), "roof-quote-mate", "estimate.js")
        script = ("const {estimate} = require(process.argv[1]); const c = JSON.parse(require('fs').readFileSync(process.argv[2], 'utf8'));"
                  "const out = []; for (const t of c.jobTypes) for (const d of c.difficulty) for (const s of c.sizes) {"
                  "const e = estimate(c, {type: t.id, difficulty: d.id, size: s.id}); out.push([t.id, d.id, s.id, e.low, e.high]); }"
                  "console.log(JSON.stringify(out));")
        checked = 0
        for name, tweak in (("roofing", {}), ("painting", {}), ("pets", {}), ("batteries", {}), ("homecare", {}), ("bookkeeping", {}),
                            ("homecare", {"gstRegistered": True, "ratesIncludeGst": True, "roundTo": 1}),
                            ("roofing", {"spread": 0.075, "roundTo": 1, "minimumJob": 0, "ratesIncludeGst": True}),
                            ("painting", {"spread": 0.333, "roundTo": 25, "gstRate": 0.125}),
                            ("pets", {"gstRegistered": True, "roundTo": 1}),
                            ("batteries", {"gstRegistered": False, "ratesIncludeGst": False, "roundTo": 1}),
                            ("batteries", {"spread": 0.173, "roundTo": 25, "rebate": {"label": "x", "perUnit": 187.5,
                                                                                         "bands": [{"upTo": 7, "share": 1}, {"upTo": 30, "share": 0.35}]}})):
            with open(os.path.join(ROOT, "presets", name + ".json"), encoding="utf-8") as fh:
                cfg = json.load(fh)
            cfg["pricing"].update(tweak)
            cfg["jobTypes"][0]["labourPerUnit"] = 61.37
            with tempfile.NamedTemporaryFile("w", suffix=".json", delete=False) as fh:
                json.dump(cfg, fh)
            try:
                rows = json.loads(subprocess.run(["node", "-e", script, tool, fh.name], capture_output=True, text=True, check=True).stdout)
            finally:
                os.unlink(fh.name)
            for t, d, s, low, high in rows:
                e = estimate(cfg, t, d, s)
                self.assertEqual((e["low"], e["high"]), (low, high), (name, tweak, t, d, s))
                checked += 1
        self.assertGreater(checked, 11 * 12)
