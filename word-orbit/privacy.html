Word Orbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Word Orbit is made by [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS] ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Word Orbit is designed to be used by children with a parent or carer, and we collect as little as possible.

Without an account

If you do not sign in, everything (learner names, progress, settings and any letter sounds you record) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.

With an account

A parent or carer can sign in with an email address to back up progress and use it on other devices. We then store:

  • the parent's email address, to send sign-in links;
  • the family's app data: learner first names, chosen picture, progress, settings and spelling lists.

Children do not create accounts and are never asked for an email address. Recorded letter sounds stay on the device and are not uploaded.

Payments

Payments for the Family Plan are handled by Stripe. We never see or store card details. We receive the plan status (for example, active or cancelled) so we can unlock the plan.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of data for marketing.
  • No tracking or analytics that profile children.
  • No contact between users.

Where data is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase in [REGION, e.g. London (UK) or Frankfurt (EU)]. Payment data is held by Stripe.

How long we keep data

We keep account data while the account is in use. If an account is unused for [24 months], or you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your family's data. "Delete all data" in the app's Grown-ups area removes learners and progress from the device and the account backup. To delete the account itself, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children's privacy

We follow the UK Age Appropriate Design Code: high privacy settings by default, minimal data, no nudges to share data, and grown-up-only areas protected by a question for adults.
