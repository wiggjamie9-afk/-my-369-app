m.mp3  d.mp3  g.mp3  o.mp3  c.mp3  k.mp3

Recording tips for the voice actor:
- Say the pure sound, short and clean: "sss" not "suh", "mmm" not "muh".
- "Stretchy" sounds can be held a little: s, m, f, l, n, r, v, z, sh, th.
- "Bouncy" sounds must be very short with no "uh" after them: t, p, k, c, ck, b, d, g.
- x is said "ks", qu is said "kw".
- About 1 second each, a quiet room, the same distance from the microphone.
- Trim the silence at the start and end. Keep the volume the same across all files.

Until a file is here (or recorded in the app's Grown-ups area), the app says a keyword
instead, for example "sun" for s.
