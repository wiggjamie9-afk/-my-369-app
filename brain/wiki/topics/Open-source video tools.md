# Open-source video tools

Open-source projects that do parts of what Forge plans to do.

## Facts
- OpenReels turns a topic into a rendered Short (script, voiceover with word timings, images, captions); its author reports MIT licence and about $0.68 per video. [source: https://github.com/SucksToBeAnik/OpenReels] [source: https://dev.to/tsensei/i-open-sourced-an-ai-pipeline-that-turns-any-topic-into-a-youtube-short-e77]
- OpenShorts cuts long videos into vertical clips with word-synced captions; MIT licence, runs in Docker. [source: https://github.com/mutonby/openshorts]
- ShortGPT automates captions and multi-language voiceovers. [source: https://github.com/SolvAI/ShortGPT]
