# AI content rules

Rules that keep the channels and the business safe.

## Facts
- Label realistic AI content; no fake engagement or bots; no mass-produced lookalike videos; only original or licensed music and characters. [source: repo CLAUDE.md] [source: chat advice 2026-10-30]
- Face, voice and lip-sync features only with the owner's likeness or recorded consent. [source: repo CLAUDE.md]
- Cold-contacting scraped businesses without consent breaks Australia's Spam Act 2003. [source: chat advice 2026-10-31]
