# 📚 Amazon KDP Setup Pack — Dad's Code

Everything to publish the books on Amazon KDP, ready to copy-paste. **You** create the KDP account (kdp.amazon.com), do the one-time tax + bank interview, upload, and hit Publish — I can't log in as you. This pack makes each book ~10 minutes of data entry.

---

## One-time account setup (do once)
1. Go to **kdp.amazon.com** → sign in with your Amazon account.
2. Complete the **Tax Interview** (Australian resident → they'll ask for your TFN/details) and **bank details** for royalties. Non-negotiable, one-time.
3. Then: **Create → Kindle eBook** (and/or **Paperback**).

## Files to upload
- **Paperback:** upload the ready-made PDFs in `podcast/dads-code/pdf/`. Trim size: use **8.5" × 11"** for journals/workbooks/cookbooks (they're designed for it); **6" × 9"** for the text books (Present Not Perfect, ADHD Playbook, Steady, 90-Day, Bedtime Stories).
- **Kindle eBook:** upload the same **PDF** (KDP auto-converts) — or paste the text into a free **Google Doc → download as .docx → upload** for a nicer reflowable Kindle version.
- **Cover:** make in Canva from the cover brief in each product's `.md`, or use KDP's free **Cover Creator**. (KDP needs a front-cover JPG for eBook; a full wrap for paperback — Cover Creator builds the wrap for you.)

## Author bio (paste into your KDP author profile / Author Central)
> Jamie Wigg is a dad of four, late-diagnosed with ADHD and dyslexia, who rebuilt his health and his life — and now helps other dads do the same through the Dad's Code podcast and books. He writes plain, honest, and from experience. Present, not perfect.

## Pricing note
KDP royalty: **70%** if you price a Kindle eBook **$2.99–$9.99** (else 35%). Paperbacks: price above the printing cost KDP shows you (usually set paperbacks ~$9.99–$14.99).

## Recommended KDP launch order
1. **Present, Not Perfect** (eBook + paperback) — flagship.
2. **Dad's Code Kitchen** (paperback + eBook) — broad appeal.
3. **The ADHD Dad's Playbook** (eBook) — strong searchable niche.
Then add the rest over the following weeks (don't do all at once).

## After publishing
- Set up **Amazon Author Central** (free) → claim your books, add the bio + a photo.
- Link Amazon listings from your podcast + socials + email.
- Ask early readers for **reviews** — the single biggest KDP lever.
