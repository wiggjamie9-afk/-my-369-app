# 🎯 Dad's Code — The Playbook
*Synthesised from 10 specialist advisors. This is the focused growth plan for ONE brand: Dad's Code.*

---

## THE BIG IDEA (repositioning)
Stop competing in crowded "funny dad life" content. Own a sharper niche:

> **The Rebuilt Dad** — a late-diagnosed (ADHD + dyslexia), grief-tested, health-transformed dad cracking the code on being **present over perfect** — for men who think it's too late to change.

- **Tagline:** *Present, not perfect.*
- **Exactly who it's for:** men 38–55, co-parenting, who feel they "left it too late" — overweight, newly/undiagnosed neurodivergent, grieving or numb. Speak TO him; make his **partner** want to tag him.
- **Your unfair advantage** (put these at the centre of every story): diagnosis at 47 · the body rebuild · losing both parents.
- **Podcast title field (for search):** `Dad's Code: Present Dad Parenting, ADHD & Fatherhood`

---

## THE 3 CONTENT PILLARS (own these)
1. **Present Over Perfect** (flagship) — imperfect micro-moments of showing up.
2. **The Late-Diagnosed Brain** — ADHD + dyslexia as an adult/dad; systems & reframes.
3. **Still Here** — grief, depression & the health turnaround; hope without toxic positivity.
4. *(test)* **Dad Tools** — tiny tactics/scripts that make presence easier.

## 🏆 TOP PRIORITY MOVES (ranked, cross-advisor consensus)

1. **UPGRADE BUZZSPROUT OFF FREE — today.** The 90-day auto-delete is *fatal* for a memoir show; your back-catalogue is the whole value. (~US$12/mo, keeps your RSS.) *Prerequisite for everything.*
2. **Publish the memoir as an eBook** (Amazon KDP + Gumroad) and use it to **build an email list.** The list — not the podcast — is what every future dollar depends on.
3. **Reposition to "The Rebuilt Dad"** and rename the podcast title field for search.
4. **Batch content on a fortnightly "Studio Day" and always keep 2 episodes banked** — so no bad day breaks the streak (ADHD-proofing).
5. **Launch the podcast with 3 episodes**, submit to Apple + Spotify + YouTube, and push hard in the **first 8 weeks** (Apple's "New & Noteworthy" window). Ask for reviews relentlessly.
6. **Confession-style hooks + one specific question per post** to drive shares, comments and DMs.

## 🎬 SHORT-FORM VIDEO (TikTok + Instagram, @dadscode)
- **You ON camera** — authenticity is the product. No AI avatar for Dad's Code.
- **Length:** mostly 15–34s; let story posts run 45–60s. Cut dead air in the first 3s.
- **Cadence:** 1/day, 5–6×/week — **batch-filmed in one sitting.**
- **What the algorithm rewards (2026):** retention → shares → saves → comments. Likes ≈ noise. Engineer "send this to a dad you know."
- **Always:** burn-in captions, big high-contrast text, out of the bottom third, one idea per card.
- **~60% evergreen story / ~40% trend-jacked.** Original audio on the deep stuff.
- **Reality:** 6–12 months to traction; most accounts need 30–50+ posts before a breakout. Don't buy followers, don't over-automate.

## 🪝 THE HOOK BANK (open on the wound, not the lesson)
Lead with the smallest TRUE concrete detail — a quote, an age, an object — and trust the viewer to feel it:
- "Tuesday never came. And I'm still waiting by the phone."
- "I was ten when I promised I'd be a different kind of dad."
- "My dad's last words to me were: 'I'll chat to you Tuesday.'"
- "They called me stupid at seven. At 47 a doctor gave it a name."
- "I blew my son a cuddle across the room — and it broke me open."
- "I lost both parents and 40 kilos. One of those saved my life."

**30–60s structure:** Hook (0–3s) → Stakes (3–10s) → Turn (10–35s) → Meaning (35–50s, the one earned line) → soft Invite (50–60s). One emotional peak per clip. End on resonance, not resolution.

## ♻️ ONE EPISODE → A WEEK OF CONTENT
From a single 18–25 min episode, cut: (1) raw hook clip, (2) the one-lesson clip, (3) a "Dad Tool" clip, (4) audiogram/quote-card, (5) a 5–7 slide carousel (great for IG saves), (6) a text-only thought post, (7) a question/reply-bait post.

## 👥 COMMUNITY
- **End every post with ONE specific, confession-style question** (not "thoughts?").
- **Reply like a human in the first 60 min** — early reply velocity boosts reach AND builds superfans.
- **Email list from day one** (own it; you only rent social). Weekly "Dad's Code letter."
- **Named UGC series** ("Real Dad Confessions") — read anonymous dad stories (with permission).
- **Pick 20–30 superfans and go deep** — they become your word-of-mouth + launch engine.

## 💰 MONETISATION (sequenced — don't rush it)
Build audience + email list for ~3–6 months first. Then:
1. **Memoir eBook** (KDP ~$4.99–6.99, ~70% royalty; + Gumroad to own the customer).
2. **Free lead magnet** ("5 Ways to Be a More Present Dad" PDF) → grows the list.
3. **Low-price products** ($7–19 "Present Dad" guide, ADHD/dad journal) — Canva, high margin.
4. **Affiliate** (passive, honest, on-brand) — small.
5. **Membership/Patreon** — only after ~50–100 true superfans (month 6+).
6. **Sponsorships** — need ~1,000+ downloads/episode; later.
7. **Speaking & coaching** — highest $/hr; your endgame, once the book + audience prove it.

## 🛟 SUSTAINABLE SYSTEM (ADHD-friendly, anti-burnout)
- **Cadence is a floor AND a ceiling:** 1 episode + 3 clips per week. "This is enough."
- **Studio Day fortnightly** → 2 episodes of audio at once. Separate **Create Day** from **Schedule Day**.
- **Automate distribution** (Buzzsprout schedule + Buffer/Metricool free), **keep the voice + replies manual.**
- **3-column board** (Ideas → In Progress → Scheduled). Nothing fancier.
- **Guardrails:** keep 2 episodes banked · written "Skip Rule" (missing a week is OK; never double up to catch up) · 90-min session cap · one paid helper (Upwork VA) for the task you most avoid · 15-min Friday shutdown review.

---

## 🛡️ SHARING SAFELY (protect yourself & your kids)
- **"Healed, not bleeding" rule:** only publish from scars you've processed. Draft raw → wait 48h → post the processed version.
- **Kids:** no real-time location/school/full names; backs-of-heads, past-tense; veto power once they're ~8+. Tell *your* story where they appear, not *theirs*.
- **Mental-health posts:** content warning + always pair dark with the turn + pin resources (Lifeline 13 11 14 · Beyond Blue 1300 22 4636 · 13YARN 13 92 76) + "my experience, not advice."
- **Parasocial boundary:** "I read messages, but I'm not a counsellor — if you're in crisis, call Lifeline." Don't run DM crisis support.
- **Comments:** keyword filters, never reply in the first sting, block freely. Have a trusted person triage heavy posts.
- **No new trauma for reach.** Schedule recovery like content; keep a therapist as the brand grows.
