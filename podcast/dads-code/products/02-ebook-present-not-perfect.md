## RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS (cite lightly inside)
- **Bids for connection** (Gottman) — relationships are built in small everyday moments.
- **Good-enough parenting** (Winnicott) — kids don't need perfect parents; they need consistent, "good-enough" ones. Repair matters more than flawlessness.
- **Rupture & repair** — secure attachment comes from *fixing* breaks, not avoiding them.
- **ADHD/dyslexia & parenting** — neurodivergent parents thrive with external systems, not willpower; shame is the real blocker.

## INTRODUCTION — The Dad I Thought I Had to Be
I grew up with a father whose love arrived as money, not presence. At ten, I made a silent vow: if I ever have kids, I'll be there. It took me decades — and a lot of falling down — to learn that "being there" was never about being perfect. This little book is the field guide I wish someone had handed me.

## CH 1 — The Myth of the Perfect Dad
The perfect-dad image is a trap; chasing it makes you *less* present, because you're performing instead of connecting. Research on "good-enough" parenting shows kids need consistency and warmth, not perfection. **Action: write down one "perfect dad" standard you're going to drop this week.**

## CH 2 — What "Present" Actually Means
Presence isn't hours logged; it's attention given. The science of "bids for connection": the small moments — "Dad, look!" — are where the bond is actually built. **Action: turn toward three bids a day, fully, for one week.**

## CH 3 — The 6-Word Reset (managing *your* storm first)
You can't be present while you're flooded. The reframe that saved me: *"They're not giving me a hard time — they're having one."* Regulate yourself first; the parenting follows. **Action: one slow breath + the six words, before you react.**

## CH 4 — When Your Brain Works Differently
Diagnosed with ADHD at 47, dyslexic my whole life — I spent years thinking I was broken. I wasn't; I just needed systems, not shame. External reminders, routines, and self-forgiveness beat willpower every time. **Action: build one "second brain" system for the thing you keep forgetting.**

## CH 5 — Repair Beats Perfect
You'll snap. You'll get it wrong. What your kids remember is whether you come back. "I got that wrong, mate. I'm sorry." Rupture-and-repair is how trust is actually built. **Action: make one real repair this week, out loud.**

## CH 6 — The Air Cuddle (small rituals, big roots)
My son whispers "I love you, Dad," and we press our ears together — the air cuddle. Tiny rituals are how presence becomes permanent. They cost nothing and outlast everything. **Action: create one two-second ritual that's just yours.**

## CLOSE — Present, Not Perfect
The cycle stops with you. You don't have to be the dad you never had overnight — you just have to show up today. Imperfectly. Present.
