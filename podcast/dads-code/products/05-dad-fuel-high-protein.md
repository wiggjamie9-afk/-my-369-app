## 6. THE ONE RULE

Aim for a **palm-sized serve of protein at every meal** — eggs, meat, fish, dairy, beans, tofu.

Protein helps you feel full, keeps your energy steadier, and protects muscle while you move more. That's about 90% of the job. Get that right most days and the rest takes care of itself. (General guide only — see *Read This First*.)

## 8. ⏱️ 15-MIN FUEL

### 🥇 Greek Yogurt Power Bowl  ⚡⏱️
**⏱️ 5 min · 🍽️ 1 · 🔋 ~25g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 1 big spoon Greek yogurt (about a cup)
- Handful of berries
- Handful of nuts
- Drizzle of honey

**👣 Steps**
1. Spoon yogurt into a bowl.
2. Top with berries and nuts.
3. Drizzle honey. Done.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Buy plain high-protein Greek yogurt and add your own honey — way more protein, way less sugar than the flavoured tubs.

---

### 🥇 Tuna & White Bean Salad  ⚡⏱️
**⏱️ 10 min · 🍽️ 1–2 · 🔋 ~35g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 1 tin tuna, drained
- 1 tin cannellini beans, drained
- 1 tomato, chopped
- Olive oil + lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

**👣 Steps**
1. Tip tuna and beans into a bowl.
2. Add tomato.
3. Dress with oil, lemon, salt and pepper. Mix.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** No cooking, massive protein, keeps in the fridge. Make double for tomorrow's lunch.

---

### 🥇 Cottage Cheese Toast, 3 Ways  ⚡⏱️
**⏱️ 5 min · 🍽️ 1 · 🔋 ~20g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 2 slices wholegrain toast
- Cottage cheese
- Pick a topper: (a) tomato + pepper · (b) banana + honey · (c) egg + chilli

**👣 Steps**
1. Toast the bread.
2. Spread cottage cheese on thick.
3. Add your topper of choice.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Cottage cheese is the most underrated protein hit going — cheap, filling, and it works sweet or savoury.

---

### 🥇 Post-Run Protein Smoothie  ⚡⏱️
**⏱️ 3 min · 🍽️ 1 · 🔋 ~30g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 1 cup milk
- 1 scoop protein powder
- 1 banana
- 1 spoon peanut butter
- Handful of oats

**👣 Steps**
1. Tip everything in a blender.
2. Blend until smooth.
3. Drink. Recovery in a cup.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Freeze the banana first for a thick, milkshake-feel smoothie with zero ice needed.

---

### 🥇 Steak & Sweet Potato  ⚡⏱️
**⏱️ 15 min · 🍽️ 1 · 🔋 ~40g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 1 steak
- 1 sweet potato
- Handful of greens
- Oil, salt, pepper

**👣 Steps**
1. Microwave the sweet potato (8–10 min) while you heat the pan.
2. Pan-sear the steak to your liking; rest it a couple of minutes.
3. Steam or microwave the greens. Plate up.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Proper dad fuel, fast. Resting the steak for 2 minutes makes it far juicier — don't skip it.

---

### 🥇 Mince & Egg Power Bowl  ⚡⏱️
**⏱️ 12 min · 🍽️ 1 · 🔋 ~38g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 150g mince
- Splash of soy sauce + garlic
- 1 egg
- Cooked rice or greens to serve

**👣 Steps**
1. Brown the mince with garlic and a splash of soy.
2. Fry an egg.
3. Serve mince over rice or greens, top with the egg.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Huge protein, dead simple. Use leftover rice from your Sunday prep and it's a 5-minute job.

---

### 🥇 Egg & Veg Scramble Wrap  ⚡⏱️
**⏱️ 8 min · 🍽️ 1 · 🔋 ~22g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 3 eggs
- Handful of spinach
- Cheese
- 1 wrap

**👣 Steps**
1. Scramble the eggs with spinach.
2. Stir in cheese to melt.
3. Roll it all up in the wrap.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Works for breakfast or a late-night refuel. Add leftover veg or mince to bulk it out.

## 9. 🍱 MEAL-PREP HEROES

### 🥇 Big-Batch Beef & Bean Chilli  ⚡🍱
**⏱️ 30 min · 🍽️ 5 · 🔋 ~35g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 500g lean beef mince
- 1 onion
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tin kidney beans
- Chilli + cumin

**👣 Steps**
1. Brown the mince with the onion.
2. Add tomatoes, beans, chilli and cumin.
3. Simmer 20 min.
4. Portion into containers — lunch sorted all week.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Freezes brilliantly. Make a double batch and you've got dinners stocked for a fortnight.

---

### 🥇 Overnight Protein Oats  ⚡🍱
**⏱️ 5 min night before · 🍽️ 1 · 🔋 ~30g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- ½ cup oats
- Milk
- Greek yogurt
- 1 scoop protein
- 1 banana

**👣 Steps**
1. Mix everything in a jar.
2. Lid on, fridge overnight.
3. Grab and go in the morning.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Steady morning energy, no sugar crash. Make 3 jars at once for a stress-free week of breakfasts.

---

### 🥇 Chicken & Rice Prep Boxes  ⚡🍱
**⏱️ 30 min · 🍽️ 4 · 🔋 ~38g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 8 chicken thighs, seasoned
- 1½ cups rice
- 1 tray of veg

**👣 Steps**
1. Bake the seasoned thighs.
2. Cook the rice.
3. Roast a tray of veg.
4. Box into four — four ready dinners.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Thighs stay juicier than breast and cost less. Season three different ways so it doesn't feel like the same meal four nights running.

---

### 🥇 Freezer Breakfast Burritos  ⚡🍱
**⏱️ 30 min · 🍽️ 6 · 🔋 ~28g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 6 eggs
- 250g mince
- Cheese
- 1 capsicum
- 6 tortillas

**👣 Steps**
1. Scramble eggs with mince, cheese and capsicum.
2. Roll the filling into the tortillas.
3. Wrap and freeze.
4. Microwave 2 min on a rough morning.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Wrap each one in foil or baking paper before freezing so they don't stick together.

---

### 🥇 Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs & Veg  ⚡🍱
**⏱️ 30 min · 🍽️ 4 · 🔋 ~36g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- Chicken thighs
- Chopped veg
- Oil, salt, pepper

**👣 Steps**
1. Spread thighs and veg on one tray.
2. Roast 25 min at 200°C.
3. Serve now; box the rest for lunch.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** One tray means one wash-up. Line it with baking paper and you've barely got that.

---

### 🥇 Lentil & Sausage One-Pot  🍱
**⏱️ 30 min · 🍽️ 4 · 🔋 ~28g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 4 good sausages
- 1 cup lentils
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- Stock

**👣 Steps**
1. Brown the sausages in the pot.
2. Add lentils, tomato and stock.
3. Simmer until thick.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Hearty, cheap and it freezes well. The lentils bump the protein and keep you full for hours.

---

### 🥇 Salmon Traybake  ⚡
**⏱️ 25 min · 🍽️ 2 · 🔋 ~34g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- 2 salmon fillets
- Tray of veg
- Lemon + olive oil

**👣 Steps**
1. Lay salmon and veg on a tray.
2. Drizzle with lemon and oil.
3. Bake 18 min.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Omega-3s plus protein for brain and body. Frozen salmon fillets are cheaper and just as good baked.

---

### 🥇 Big-Batch Chicken Soup  ⚡🍱
**⏱️ 30 min · 🍽️ 6 · 🔋 ~30g protein**

**🛒 Ingredients**
- Chicken (thighs or a small whole bird)
- Stock
- Veg
- Handful of pasta or rice

**👣 Steps**
1. Simmer chicken in stock with the veg.
2. Add pasta or rice near the end.
3. Cool and portion.

> 💡 **Fuel Tip:** Freezes for weeks — medicine on a hard day. Shred extra chicken into wraps or salads through the week.

## 11. THE 1-HOUR SUNDAY PREP SYSTEM

In about **60 minutes** you can set up your whole week — so tired-you doesn't have to decide anything.

**The flow:**
1. **Bake** a tray of chicken + a tray of veg.
2. **Cook** a pot of rice + a batch of chilli or soup.
3. **Boil** 6 eggs.
4. **Box** it into 4–5 lunches/dinners.

**🗒️ Sunday Prep Checklist** *(Canva: render as a tick-box graphic — amber checkboxes on a charcoal card, one item per row, big tap-friendly boxes):*

- [ ] Chicken tray in the oven
- [ ] Veg tray in the oven
- [ ] Rice on the stove
- [ ] Chilli or soup simmering
- [ ] 6 eggs boiling
- [ ] Containers out and labelled
- [ ] Everything boxed and in the fridge

> 💡 **System Tip:** Do it while the kids are busy or the game's on. One hour now buys you a week of "it's already done."
