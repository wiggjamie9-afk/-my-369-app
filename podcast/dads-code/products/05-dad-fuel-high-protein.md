## THE ONE RULE
Aim to get a **palm-sized serve of protein at every meal** (eggs, meat, fish, dairy, beans). Protein keeps you full, steadies your energy, and protects muscle. That's 90% of it.

Look for: ⚡ = high protein · 🍱 = meal-preps/freezes well · ⏱️ = under 15 min.

## THE RECIPES

### 1. Big-Batch Beef & Bean Chilli ⚡🍱
*30 min · makes 5 serves.* Brown 500g lean mince + onion, add tinned tomatoes, kidney beans, chilli/cumin, simmer 20 min. Portion into containers — lunch sorted all week.

### 2. Overnight Protein Oats ⚡🍱
*5 min night before.* Oats + milk + Greek yogurt + scoop of protein + banana. Grab and go. Steady morning energy, no sugar crash.

### 3. Greek Yogurt Power Bowl ⚡⏱️
*5 min.* Big spoon of Greek yogurt, berries, handful of nuts, drizzle of honey. ~25g protein in two minutes.

### 4. Chicken & Rice Prep Boxes ⚡🍱
*30 min · makes 4.* Bake seasoned chicken thighs, cook rice, roast a tray of veg. Box them up — four ready dinners.

### 5. Tuna & White Bean Salad ⚡⏱️
*10 min.* Tinned tuna + cannellini beans + tomato + olive oil + lemon. No cooking, massive protein.

### 6. Freezer Breakfast Burritos ⚡🍱
*30 min · makes 6.* Scramble eggs with mince + cheese + capsicum, roll in tortillas, freeze. Microwave 2 min on a rough morning.

### 7. Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs & Veg ⚡🍱
*30 min.* Thighs + chopped veg on one tray, 25 min at 200°C. Minimal effort, leftovers for lunch.

### 8. Cottage Cheese Toast, 3 Ways ⚡⏱️
*5 min.* Wholegrain toast + cottage cheese, then: (a) tomato + pepper, (b) banana + honey, (c) egg + chilli. Underrated protein hit.

### 9. Post-Run Protein Smoothie ⚡⏱️
*3 min.* Milk + protein powder + banana + peanut butter + oats. Blend. Recovery in a cup.

### 10. Lentil & Sausage One-Pot 🍱
*30 min.* Brown good sausages, add lentils, tinned tomato, stock, simmer. Hearty, cheap, freezes well.

### 11. Steak & Sweet Potato ⚡⏱️
*15 min.* Pan-sear a steak, microwave a sweet potato, steam greens. Proper dad fuel, fast.

### 12. Mince & Egg Power Bowl ⚡⏱️
*12 min.* Brown mince with soy + garlic, top with a fried egg, serve on rice or greens. Huge protein, dead simple.

### 13. Salmon Traybake ⚡
*25 min.* Salmon fillets + veg on a tray, lemon + olive oil, 18 min. Omega-3s + protein for brain and body.

### 14. Big-Batch Chicken Soup ⚡🍱
*30 min · makes 6.* Chicken, stock, veg, a handful of pasta or rice. Freezes for weeks; medicine on a hard day.

### 15. Egg & Veg Scramble Wrap ⚡⏱️
*8 min.* 3 eggs scrambled with spinach + cheese in a wrap. Fast breakfast or late-night refuel.

## THE 1-HOUR SUNDAY PREP (the system)
In ~60 minutes you can set up your week:
1. **Bake** a tray of chicken + a tray of veg.
2. **Cook** a pot of rice + a batch of chilli or soup.
3. **Boil** 6 eggs.
4. **Box** it into 4–5 lunches/dinners.
Now tired-you doesn't have to decide anything.
