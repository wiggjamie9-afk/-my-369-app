## ⚡ 15-MIN HEROES

### 1. Scrambled Egg & Ham Wraps ⚡🙌
**⏱ 10 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~18g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 8 eggs
- Splash of milk
- 4 slices ham, chopped
- Grated cheese
- 4 tortillas
- Butter, salt

**Steps**
1. Whisk eggs with milk and a pinch of salt.
2. Soft-scramble in buttered pan over low heat.
3. Stir ham and cheese through at the end.
4. Spoon into tortillas, roll, and serve.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Low and slow = soft eggs. Pull them off the heat while still glossy.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Whisking the eggs and rolling the wraps.

---

### 2. Cheesy Tuna Melt Boats ⚡
**⏱ 15 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~22g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 2 tins tuna, drained
- 2 tbsp mayo
- 1/2 cup corn
- 4 bread rolls, halved
- Grated cheese

**Steps**
1. Mix tuna, mayo and corn.
2. Hollow the rolls slightly and pile the mix in.
3. Top with cheese.
4. Grill 3–4 min until bubbling and golden.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Cheap, fast, big protein hit — keep tinned tuna in the cupboard for emergencies.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Filling the boats with the tuna mix.

---

### 3. Chicken & Cheese Quesadilla Triangles ⚡🙌
**⏱ 15 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~24g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
- Grated cheese
- 8 tortillas
- Salsa or sour cream, to serve

**Steps**
1. Layer chicken and cheese between two tortillas.
2. Pan-fry both sides until golden and the cheese melts.
3. Cut into triangles.
4. Serve with salsa or sour cream for dunking.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Use leftover roast chook or a supermarket BBQ chicken to skip the cooking.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Sprinkling cheese and cutting triangles (with a kid-safe knife).

---

### 4. Crunchy Fish Finger Wraps ⚡
**⏱ 15 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~16g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 12 oven fish fingers
- 4 wraps
- Shredded lettuce
- Grated cheese
- Mayo

**Steps**
1. Bake fish fingers per packet.
2. Smear mayo on each wrap.
3. Add lettuce, cheese and 3 fish fingers.
4. Roll up and serve handheld.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Handheld = happy kids and almost no plates to wash.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Building and rolling their own wrap.

## 🍽️ FAMILY FAVOURITES

### 5. Build-Your-Own Taco Night ⚡🙌
**⏱ 20 min · 👥 4–5 · 💪 ~26g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 500g beef mince
- 1 sachet taco seasoning
- Taco shells
- Grated cheese, lettuce, tomato, corn

**Steps**
1. Brown the mince, breaking it up as it cooks.
2. Stir in taco seasoning and a splash of water; simmer 3 min.
3. Lay out shells and toppings on the table.
4. Everyone builds their own.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Lean into the chaos — letting them build it is half the fun.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Assembling tacos and laying out the topping bowls.

---

### 6. Speedy Spag Bol ⚡
**⏱ 30 min · 👥 4–6 · 💪 ~25g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 500g beef mince
- 1 jar pasta sauce
- 1 carrot + 1 zucchini, grated
- 400g spaghetti
- Grated cheese, to serve

**Steps**
1. Brown the mince.
2. Stir in the grated veg and the jar sauce; simmer 15 min.
3. Cook spaghetti until al dente.
4. Combine and top with cheese.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Make a double batch — it freezes brilliantly for next week's solo night.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Grating the hidden veg (carrot and zucchini).

---

### 7. Sheet-Pan Sausages & Veg
**⏱ 30 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~20g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 8 sausages
- 3 potatoes, chopped
- 1 capsicum, chopped
- 1 onion, wedged
- Olive oil, salt

**Steps**
1. Heat oven to 200°C.
2. Toss everything on one tray with oil and salt.
3. Bake 25 min, turning once.
4. Serve straight from the tray.

👨 **Dad Tip:** One tray, almost no washing up. Line it with baking paper.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Tossing the veg in oil before it goes in.

---

### 8. Honey Soy Drumsticks ⚡
**⏱ 30 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~28g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 12 chicken drumsticks
- 3 tbsp honey
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed

**Steps**
1. Heat oven to 200°C.
2. Toss drumsticks in honey, soy and garlic.
3. Spread on a lined tray.
4. Bake 25 min until sticky and cooked through.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Spoon the tray juices back over halfway for extra sticky glaze.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Mixing the marinade and tossing the drumsticks (then washing hands!).

---

### 9. Loaded Sweet Potato Boats ⚡
**⏱ 30 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~24g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 4 sweet potatoes
- 400g beef mince, seasoned
- Grated cheese

**Steps**
1. Microwave sweet potatoes 8 min until soft.
2. Brown and season the mince.
3. Split potatoes, top with mince and cheese.
4. Grill 3 min until cheese melts.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Microwave first, oven last — that's how you beat the 30-minute clock.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Sprinkling cheese on top of the boats.

## 🙌 FUN HANDS-ON

### 10. Loaded Baked Chicken Nuggets ⚡🙌
**⏱ 25 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~30g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 600g chicken breast, cut into bites
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 2 cups cornflakes, crushed
- 1/2 cup parmesan
- Oven chips + carrot sticks, to serve

**Steps**
1. Heat oven to 200°C.
2. Dip chicken in egg, then in cornflake–parmesan crumb.
3. Spread on a lined tray.
4. Bake 18 min until golden and cooked through.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Set up an egg bowl and a crumb bowl — assembly-line crumbing keeps the mess in one place.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Crushing the cornflakes and crumbing the chicken.

---

### 11. Mini Meatball Subs ⚡🙌
**⏱ 30 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~27g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 500g beef/pork mince
- 1 jar pasta sauce
- 4 small bread rolls
- Grated cheese

**Steps**
1. Roll mince into mini meatballs.
2. Pan-fry until browned.
3. Simmer in pasta sauce 10 min.
4. Tuck into rolls and top with cheese.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Wet your hands before rolling so the mince doesn't stick.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Rolling the meatballs.

---

### 12. DIY Pizza Pitas 🙌
**⏱ 20 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~14g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 4 pita breads
- Pizza/pasta sauce
- Grated cheese
- Toppings of their choice

**Steps**
1. Heat oven to 200°C.
2. Spread sauce on each pita.
3. Add cheese and chosen toppings.
4. Bake 10 min until crisp.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Add a few slices of ham or leftover chicken for a bigger protein hit.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Designing their own face or animal pizza.

---

### 13. Rainbow Egg Muffins ⚡
**⏱ 25 min (make-ahead) · 👥 4 (12 muffins) · 💪 ~16g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 8 eggs
- 1 capsicum, chopped
- 4 slices ham, chopped
- 1/2 cup peas
- Grated cheese

**Steps**
1. Heat oven to 180°C; grease a muffin tin.
2. Whisk eggs, stir in capsicum, ham, peas and cheese.
3. Pour into tin cups.
4. Bake 18 min until set.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Great hot or cold — make a batch for breakfast-for-dinner or lunchboxes.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Choosing and stirring in the "rainbow" fillings.

---

### 14. Crispy Chickpea & Cheese Nachos ⚡
**⏱ 20 min · 👥 4 · 💪 ~15g protein/serve**

**Ingredients**
- 1 large bag corn chips
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained
- Grated cheese
- Salsa, to serve

**Steps**
1. Heat oven to 200°C.
2. Roast chickpeas 10 min until crisp.
3. Spread corn chips on a tray, scatter chickpeas and cheese.
4. Grill until melted; top with salsa.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Veggie protein and big crunch — pat the chickpeas dry before roasting for max crispness.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Scattering chickpeas and cheese over the chips.

## 🍫 SWEET TREATS

### 15. Peanut Butter Banana "Sushi" 🙌
**⏱ 5 min · 👥 4 · 💪 treat**

**Ingredients**
- 4 tortillas
- Peanut butter
- 4 bananas

**Steps**
1. Spread peanut butter on each tortilla.
2. Place a peeled banana at one edge.
3. Roll up tightly.
4. Slice into bite-size "sushi" rounds.

👨 **Dad Tip:** Five-minute treat or after-school win.
🧒 **Kid Job:** Spreading, rolling and slicing (kid-safe knife).
⚠️ **Nut allergy:** swap peanut butter for cream cheese.
