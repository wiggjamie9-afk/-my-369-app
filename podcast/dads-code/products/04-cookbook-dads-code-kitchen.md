## THE RECIPES

### 1. Loaded Baked Chicken Nuggets ⚡🎉
*25 min · ~30g protein/serve.* Chicken breast cut into bites, dipped in egg, then crushed cornflakes + parmesan, baked 18 min at 200°C. Kids help with the crumbing. Serve with oven chips + carrot sticks.

### 2. Build-Your-Own Taco Night ⚡🎉
*20 min.* Brown 500g beef mince with taco seasoning. Lay out shells, grated cheese, lettuce, tomato, corn. Everyone builds their own — chaos, but the good kind.

### 3. Cheesy Tuna Melt Boats ⚡
*15 min.* Tuna + a little mayo + corn, piled into halved bread rolls, topped with cheese, grilled till bubbling. Cheap, fast, big protein hit.

### 4. Mini Meatball Subs ⚡🎉
*30 min.* Roll beef/pork mince into mini balls, pan-fry, simmer in pasta sauce, tuck into small rolls with cheese. Kids roll the meatballs.

### 5. Rainbow Egg Muffins ⚡
*25 min (make-ahead).* Whisk 8 eggs, stir in chopped capsicum, ham, cheese, peas. Pour into muffin tin, bake 18 min. Great hot or cold — breakfast-for-dinner win.

### 6. Sheet-Pan Sausages & Veg
*30 min.* Sausages + chopped potato, capsicum, onion on one tray, olive oil, bake 25 min. One tray, almost no washing up.

### 7. Chicken & Cheese Quesadilla Triangles ⚡🎉
*15 min.* Cooked chicken + cheese between tortillas, pan-fry both sides, cut into triangles. Dunk in salsa or sour cream.

### 8. Speedy Spag Bol ⚡
*30 min.* Classic mince + jar sauce + hidden grated carrot/zucchini. Big batch, freezes brilliantly.

### 9. DIY Pizza Pitas 🎉
*20 min.* Pita base, sauce, cheese, their choice of toppings. Bake 10 min. Kids design their own face/animal pizzas.

### 10. Crunchy Fish Finger Wraps ⚡
*15 min.* Oven fish fingers in a wrap with lettuce, cheese, a smear of mayo. Handheld = happy kids.

### 11. Honey Soy Drumsticks ⚡
*30 min.* Drumsticks tossed in honey + soy + garlic, baked 25 min. Sticky, fun to eat with hands.

### 12. Loaded Sweet Potato Boats ⚡
*30 min.* Microwave sweet potatoes 8 min, split, top with seasoned mince + cheese, grill 3 min.

### 13. Scrambled Egg & Ham Wraps ⚡🎉
*10 min.* Soft scrambled eggs + ham + cheese rolled in a tortilla. The fastest dinner you'll ever make.

### 14. Crispy Chickpea & Cheese Nachos ⚡
*20 min.* Corn chips, tinned chickpeas (roasted 10 min), cheese, grill, top with salsa. Veggie protein, big crunch.

### 15. Peanut Butter Banana "Sushi" 🎉
*5 min (treat).* Wrap of peanut butter + banana, rolled and sliced into "sushi." ⚠️ *Nut allergy: swap for cream cheese.*
