## RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS
- **Habit formation** — small, daily, cued actions beat big intentions. A journal is the cue.
- **Reflection improves follow-through** — writing a tiny reflection locks the behaviour in.
- **Behaviour change works best when it's tiny** (BJ Fogg's "Tiny Habits"): one small win a day compounds.

---

## HOW TO USE (intro page)
One page a day. Each has a **micro-action** (do it today) and a **one-line reflection** (fill it at night). Two minutes. That's the whole deal. Don't aim for perfect — aim for present. Miss a day? Just start the next one. — *Jamie, Dad's Code*

## DAILY TEMPLATE (repeat for each day)
> **Day __**
> **Today's micro-action:** [prompt]
> **Tonight, one line:** What happened when I tried it? ____________________
> **Present, not perfect.**

## THE 30 PROMPTS
1. Give one child 5 minutes of your full, phone-free attention.
2. Say "I love you" first, before they do.
3. Turn fully toward the next "Dad, look at this!"
4. Apologise to your kid for something — out loud.
5. Ask one child: "What was the best part of your day?" and just listen.
6. Put your phone in another room for the whole evening routine.
7. Make up a silly song or handshake together.
8. Catch yourself before one snap — breathe, then respond.
9. Tell one kid one specific thing you're proud of (not "good job").
10. Let them choose the activity, and don't fix how they do it.
11. Sit on the floor and play *their* way for 10 minutes.
12. Share one small story from when you were their age.
13. Do the bedtime routine slowly tonight — no rushing.
14. Ask: "Is there anything you wish I did differently?" Just listen.
15. Give a two-minute cuddle with zero agenda.
16. Eat one meal with no screens, for anyone, including you.
17. Say sorry to your partner in front of the kids (modelling repair).
18. Write your child a tiny note and leave it where they'll find it.
19. When they melt down, ask yourself: "What are they really telling me?"
20. Do one chore *together* instead of alone.
21. Tell them about a time you failed and what you learned.
22. Put work away 15 minutes earlier than usual today.
23. Name a feeling out loud: "I'm feeling stressed, so I'm taking a breath."
24. Ask one open question at dinner and let the silence sit.
25. Create one new tiny ritual (an "air cuddle" of your own).
26. Praise effort, not result, once today.
27. Let one small mess go without commenting.
28. Look them in the eyes and say one thing you love about them.
29. Do the thing they always ask for that you usually say "later" to.
30. Reflect: what changed in 30 days? Write three lines and keep going.
