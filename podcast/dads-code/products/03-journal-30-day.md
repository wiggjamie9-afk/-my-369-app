## 3. PAGE LAYOUT SPECS

Design system across every page: charcoal headers/accents on off-white paper, one amber rule per page, lots of white space, left-aligned text, 13–14pt body min.

### Title page
Centered. Title in amber rounded serif, subtitle below in charcoal. Thin amber rule. Small footer: *by Jamie Wigg · Dad's Code · Present, not perfect.*

### "How to use" page
> One page a day. Each page gives you a **micro-action** to do today and **one line to write** tonight. Two minutes — that's the whole deal.
> Don't aim for perfect. Aim for **present**. Miss a day? Just start the next one. No catch-up, no guilt.
> — *Jamie, Dad's Code*

Below: a tiny labelled diagram of the daily page so the buyer knows exactly how each day works.

### Daily page template (repeats ×30)
Clean, consistent, low-ink. One day per page:

```
  DAY __                                          ● ● ● (week dots)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────  (amber rule)

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  TODAY'S MICRO-ACTION                        │
  │  [ prompt text, large, easy to read ]        │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘   ← prompt box

  ☐  I did it today.                                 ← action checkbox

  TONIGHT, ONE LINE
  What happened when I tried it?
  ____________________________________________       ← reflection line

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Present, not perfect.                              ← footer tagline
```

- **Prompt box:** thin amber outline, rounded corners, the day's action inside.
- **Checkbox:** single square — one decisive "done" tick.
- **Reflection line:** one ruled line only (keeps it 2-minute, low-pressure).
- **Footer:** tagline in small caps, muted.

### Weekly check-in page (after Days 7, 14, 21, 28)
Light reflective interlude, same calm styling:
- *What's one moment from this week I want to remember?* (3 lines)
- *What got easier?* (1 line)
- *One thing to carry into next week:* (1 line)
- Footer: *You showed up this week. That's the code.*

### Final reflection page (after Day 30)
- *What changed in 30 days?* (4 lines)
- *What did my kid get from this that I didn't expect?* (2 lines)
- *One ritual I'm keeping for good:* (1 line)
- Close: **You showed up for 30 days. Not perfectly — present. That's the code.**

## 4. THE 30 PROMPTS (mapped to pages)

**Week 1 — Show up (Days 1–7)**
1. Give one child 5 minutes of your full, phone-free attention.
2. Say "I love you" first, before they do.
3. Turn fully toward the next "Dad, look at this!"
4. Apologise to your kid for something — out loud.
5. Ask one child, "What was the best part of your day?" and just listen.
6. Put your phone in another room for the whole evening routine.
7. Make up a silly song or handshake together.
> *Weekly check-in →*

**Week 2 — Slow down (Days 8–14)**
8. Catch yourself before one snap — breathe, then respond.
9. Tell one kid one specific thing you're proud of (not "good job").
10. Let them choose the activity, and don't fix how they do it.
11. Sit on the floor and play *their* way for 10 minutes.
12. Share one small story from when you were their age.
13. Do the bedtime routine slowly tonight — no rushing.
14. Ask, "Is there anything you wish I did differently?" Just listen.
> *Weekly check-in →*

**Week 3 — Repair & connect (Days 15–21)**
15. Give a two-minute cuddle with zero agenda.
16. Eat one meal with no screens — for anyone, including you.
17. Say sorry to your partner in front of the kids (modelling repair).
18. Write your child a tiny note and leave it where they'll find it.
19. When they melt down, ask yourself, "What are they really telling me?"
20. Do one chore *together* instead of alone.
21. Tell them about a time you failed and what you learned.
> *Weekly check-in →*

**Week 4 — Make it stick (Days 22–28)**
22. Put work away 15 minutes earlier than usual today.
23. Name a feeling out loud: "I'm feeling stressed, so I'm taking a breath."
24. Ask one open question at dinner and let the silence sit.
25. Create one new tiny ritual (your own "air cuddle").
26. Praise effort, not result, once today.
27. Let one small mess go without commenting.
28. Look them in the eyes and say one thing you love about them.
> *Weekly check-in →*

**Finish strong (Days 29–30)**
29. Do the thing they always ask for that you usually say "later" to.
30. Reflect: what changed in 30 days? Write three lines — and keep going.
> *Final reflection →*

## 6. PRINT SPECS

- **Page size:** A4 **and** US Letter (export both; US Letter is required for KDP).
- **KDP paperback trim:** 8.5" × 11" low-content interior.
- **Margins:** 0.75" outer / 0.5" top-bottom for PDF; add a 0.125"+ gutter for the KDP version so binding doesn't eat text.
- **Print-friendly / low-ink:** off-white or plain-white pages, charcoal text, one thin amber rule per page. No full-bleed colour, no dark fills — keeps home-printing cheap.
- **Fonts embedded** in the PDF; export at 300 DPI for print, flattened.
