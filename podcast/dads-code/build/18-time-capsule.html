Present, not perfect.

Before it's forgotten

Here's the thing nobody warns you about: the good bits vanish. The made-up word for "helicopter." The way they said "I love you the whole sky." The obsession with dinosaurs that lasted exactly one summer. You swear you'll never forget — and then you do.

This is a five-minute-a-month cure. Not a diary you have to keep up with. Just a handful of prompts, once a month, capturing who your kid is right now. Fill in what you can, skip what you can't. At the end of the year, hit print, and you've got a keepsake that means more than any photo dump.

— Jamie, Dad's Code
