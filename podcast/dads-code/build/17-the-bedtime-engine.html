▶ Want fully unique AI stories? (optional)

The tool above works forever, free, with no account. But if you already have your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, you can paste it here and each story will be freshly written by an AI instead of assembled from the built-in library.

🔒 Your key stays on your device. It's saved only in this browser (localStorage) and is sent directly to the AI provider you choose when you press the button — never to Dad's Code or anyone else. Clear it any time.

No key? No problem — leave this closed and the built-in engine handles everything.
