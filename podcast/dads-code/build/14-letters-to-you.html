Present, not perfect.

A word from Jamie

Here's the thing nobody tells you: your kid is going to face the biggest days of their life on days you can't script. Their first heartbreak. The night before their wedding. The moment they hold their own baby and suddenly understand you. A hard morning, years from now, when they need someone in their corner.

You can't be everywhere. But your words can. A letter written today and opened years from now is a strange kind of time travel — it lets you sit beside them when it counts, even if you're busy, even if you're far away, even if you're gone.

Don't overthink the writing. You're not trying to be profound. You're trying to be present — honest, specific, yours. Say the ordinary true things. Those are the ones they'll read a hundred times.

Write one now. Then another when it comes to you. When you're ready, print the lot into a keepsake you can tuck away for the right day.

— Jamie
