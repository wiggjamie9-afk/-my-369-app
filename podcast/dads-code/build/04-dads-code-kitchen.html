Intro — from Jamie

The kitchen's mine tonight.

When my wife works, the kitchen's mine — four kids, one tired dad, and the clock ticking toward meltdown o'clock. I'm no chef. But I've learned dinner doesn't have to be fancy — it has to be fast, fun, and full of the good stuff that keeps everyone (including you) going.

These are my go-to, 30-minutes-or-less, kid-approved meals. Everyday ingredients, clear numbered steps, a Dad Tip to save you, and a job for the kids on every card. Make them your own.

01 Scrambled Egg & Ham Wraps ⚡ Protein🙌 Kids

⏱Time10 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~18g / serve
📷Photo slotSoft eggs, rolled wraps, dad's hands in frame

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • Splash of milk
  • 4 slices ham, chopped
  • Grated cheese
  • 4 tortillas
  • Butter, salt

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs with milk and a pinch of salt.
  2. Soft-scramble in a buttered pan over low heat.
  3. Stir ham and cheese through at the end.
  4. Spoon into tortillas, roll, and serve.
👨Dad Tip

Low and slow = soft eggs. Pull them off the heat while still glossy.

🧒Kid Job

Whisking the eggs and rolling the wraps.

⚡ 15-Min Heroes · Recipe 02

02 Cheesy Tuna Melt Boats ⚡ Protein

⏱Time15 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~22g / serve
📷Photo slotGolden bubbling cheese on toasted rolls

Ingredients

  • 2 tins tuna, drained
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • ½ cup corn
  • 4 bread rolls, halved
  • Grated cheese

Steps

  1. Mix tuna, mayo and corn.
  2. Hollow the rolls slightly and pile the mix in.
  3. Top with cheese.
  4. Grill 3–4 min until bubbling and golden.
👨Dad Tip

Cheap, fast, big protein hit — keep tinned tuna in the cupboard for emergencies.

🧒Kid Job

Filling the boats with the tuna mix.

⚡ 15-Min Heroes · Recipe 03

03 Chicken & Cheese Quesadilla Triangles ⚡ Protein🙌 Kids

⏱Time15 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~24g / serve
📷Photo slotCrispy triangles, cheese pull, salsa for dunking

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • Grated cheese
  • 8 tortillas
  • Salsa or sour cream, to serve

Steps

  1. Layer chicken and cheese between two tortillas.
  2. Pan-fry both sides until golden and the cheese melts.
  3. Cut into triangles.
  4. Serve with salsa or sour cream for dunking.
👨Dad Tip

Use leftover roast chook or a supermarket BBQ chicken to skip the cooking.

🧒Kid Job

Sprinkling cheese and cutting triangles (with a kid-safe knife).

⚡ 15-Min Heroes · Recipe 04

04 Crunchy Fish Finger Wraps ⚡ Protein

⏱Time15 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~16g / serve
📷Photo slotHandheld wrap, crunchy fish fingers peeking out

Ingredients

  • 12 oven fish fingers
  • 4 wraps
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Grated cheese
  • Mayo

Steps

  1. Bake fish fingers per packet.
  2. Smear mayo on each wrap.
  3. Add lettuce, cheese and 3 fish fingers.
  4. Roll up and serve handheld.
👨Dad Tip

Handheld = happy kids and almost no plates to wash.

🧒Kid Job

Building and rolling their own wrap.

05 Build-Your-Own Taco Night ⚡ Protein🙌 Kids

⏱Time20 min 👥Serves4–5 💪Protein~26g / serve
📷Photo slotBowls of toppings, shells lined up, kids assembling

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 sachet taco seasoning
  • Taco shells
  • Grated cheese, lettuce, tomato, corn

Steps

  1. Brown the mince, breaking it up as it cooks.
  2. Stir in taco seasoning and a splash of water; simmer 3 min.
  3. Lay out shells and toppings on the table.
  4. Everyone builds their own.
👨Dad Tip

Lean into the chaos — letting them build it is half the fun.

🧒Kid Job

Assembling tacos and laying out the topping bowls.

🍽️ Family Favourites · Recipe 06

06 Speedy Spag Bol ⚡ Protein

⏱Time30 min 👥Serves4–6 💪Protein~25g / serve
📷Photo slotTwirled spaghetti, rich sauce, grated cheese on top

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 jar pasta sauce
  • 1 carrot + 1 zucchini, grated
  • 400g spaghetti
  • Grated cheese, to serve

Steps

  1. Brown the mince.
  2. Stir in the grated veg and the jar sauce; simmer 15 min.
  3. Cook spaghetti until al dente.
  4. Combine and top with cheese.
👨Dad Tip

Make a double batch — it freezes brilliantly for next week's solo night.

🧒Kid Job

Grating the hidden veg (carrot and zucchini).

🍽️ Family Favourites · Recipe 07

07 Sheet-Pan Sausages & Veg

⏱Time30 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~20g / serve
📷Photo slotOne tray, browned sausages and roasted veg, straight from oven

Ingredients

  • 8 sausages
  • 3 potatoes, chopped
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 1 onion, wedged
  • Olive oil, salt

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 200°C.
  2. Toss everything on one tray with oil and salt.
  3. Bake 25 min, turning once.
  4. Serve straight from the tray.
👨Dad Tip

One tray, almost no washing up. Line it with baking paper.

🧒Kid Job

Tossing the veg in oil before it goes in.

🍽️ Family Favourites · Recipe 08

08 Honey Soy Drumsticks ⚡ Protein

⏱Time30 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~28g / serve
📷Photo slotSticky glazed drumsticks, glossy on the tray — hero shot

Ingredients

  • 12 chicken drumsticks
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 200°C.
  2. Toss drumsticks in honey, soy and garlic.
  3. Spread on a lined tray.
  4. Bake 25 min until sticky and cooked through.
👨Dad Tip

Spoon the tray juices back over halfway for extra sticky glaze.

🧒Kid Job

Mixing the marinade and tossing the drumsticks (then washing hands!).

🍽️ Family Favourites · Recipe 09

09 Loaded Sweet Potato Boats ⚡ Protein

⏱Time30 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~24g / serve
📷Photo slotSplit sweet potatoes, mince piled high, melted cheese

Ingredients

  • 4 sweet potatoes
  • 400g beef mince, seasoned
  • Grated cheese

Steps

  1. Microwave sweet potatoes 8 min until soft.
  2. Brown and season the mince.
  3. Split potatoes, top with mince and cheese.
  4. Grill 3 min until cheese melts.
👨Dad Tip

Microwave first, oven last — that's how you beat the 30-minute clock.

🧒Kid Job

Sprinkling cheese on top of the boats.

🙌 Fun Hands-On · Recipe 10

10 Loaded Baked Chicken Nuggets ⚡ Protein🙌 Kids

⏱Time25 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~30g / serve
📷Photo slotGolden crumbed nuggets, oven chips, carrot sticks

Ingredients

  • 600g chicken breast, cut into bites
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2 cups cornflakes, crushed
  • ½ cup parmesan
  • Oven chips + carrot sticks, to serve

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 200°C.
  2. Dip chicken in egg, then in cornflake–parmesan crumb.
  3. Spread on a lined tray.
  4. Bake 18 min until golden and cooked through.
👨Dad Tip

Set up an egg bowl and a crumb bowl — assembly-line crumbing keeps the mess in one place.

🧒Kid Job

Crushing the cornflakes and crumbing the chicken.

🙌 Fun Hands-On · Recipe 11

11 Mini Meatball Subs ⚡ Protein🙌 Kids

⏱Time30 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~27g / serve
📷Photo slotSaucy meatballs tucked in rolls, melted cheese on top

Ingredients

  • 500g beef/pork mince
  • 1 jar pasta sauce
  • 4 small bread rolls
  • Grated cheese

Steps

  1. Roll mince into mini meatballs.
  2. Pan-fry until browned.
  3. Simmer in pasta sauce 10 min.
  4. Tuck into rolls and top with cheese.
👨Dad Tip

Wet your hands before rolling so the mince doesn't stick.

🧒Kid Job

Rolling the meatballs.

🙌 Fun Hands-On · Recipe 12

12 DIY Pizza Pitas 🙌 Kids

⏱Time20 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~14g / serve
📷Photo slotPita pizzas with kid-designed faces, fresh from oven

Ingredients

  • 4 pita breads
  • Pizza/pasta sauce
  • Grated cheese
  • Toppings of their choice

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 200°C.
  2. Spread sauce on each pita.
  3. Add cheese and chosen toppings.
  4. Bake 10 min until crisp.
👨Dad Tip

Add a few slices of ham or leftover chicken for a bigger protein hit.

🧒Kid Job

Designing their own face or animal pizza.

🙌 Fun Hands-On · Recipe 13

13 Rainbow Egg Muffins ⚡ Protein

⏱Time25 min · make-ahead 👥Serves4 (12 muffins) 💪Protein~16g / serve
📷Photo slotMuffin tin of colourful egg cups, flecked with veg & ham

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 4 slices ham, chopped
  • ½ cup peas
  • Grated cheese

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 180°C; grease a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk eggs, stir in capsicum, ham, peas and cheese.
  3. Pour into tin cups.
  4. Bake 18 min until set.
👨Dad Tip

Great hot or cold — make a batch for breakfast-for-dinner or lunchboxes.

🧒Kid Job

Choosing and stirring in the "rainbow" fillings.

🙌 Fun Hands-On · Recipe 14

14 Crispy Chickpea & Cheese Nachos ⚡ Protein

⏱Time20 min 👥Serves4 💪Protein~15g / serve
📷Photo slotLoaded nacho tray, crispy chickpeas, melted cheese, salsa

Ingredients

  • 1 large bag corn chips
  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained
  • Grated cheese
  • Salsa, to serve

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 200°C.
  2. Roast chickpeas 10 min until crisp.
  3. Spread corn chips on a tray, scatter chickpeas and cheese.
  4. Grill until melted; top with salsa.
👨Dad Tip

Veggie protein and big crunch — pat the chickpeas dry before roasting for max crispness.

🧒Kid Job

Scattering chickpeas and cheese over the chips.

15 Peanut Butter Banana "Sushi" 🙌 Kids

⏱Time5 min 👥Serves4 💪ProteinTreat
📷Photo slotRolled banana "sushi" rounds on a board, kid-friendly

Ingredients

  • 4 tortillas
  • Peanut butter
  • 4 bananas

Steps

  1. Spread peanut butter on each tortilla.
  2. Place a peeled banana at one edge.
  3. Roll up tightly.
  4. Slice into bite-size "sushi" rounds.
👨Dad Tip

Five-minute treat or after-school win.

🧒Kid Job

Spreading, rolling and slicing (kid-safe knife).

⚠️Nut Allergy

Swap peanut butter for cream cheese.
