# SECTION A — Episode 1 per show: "Power of Perspective"

## True Crime Brief — "Why We're Drawn to the Darkest Stories"
**Script:**
> Welcome to True Crime Brief. Before we get into the case… let's ask a strange question. Why are we so fascinated by the worst things people do? It's not that we're morbid. Psychologists think it's the opposite — true crime is how our brains rehearse danger from a safe distance. We're not enjoying the harm… we're studying it. Learning the warning signs. Asking, 'What would I have done?' So as we go deeper this season, keep that lens. Behind every case is a human story — a victim who mattered, a moment that turned. Understanding the darkness isn't about fear. It's about protecting the light. Let's begin.

**Description:** Why are we so gripped by true crime? We unpack the psychology behind our fascination — and why studying the darkness is really about protecting the light.

## AI Briefing — "What AI Still Can't Do"
> Welcome back to AI Briefing. The hype says AI can do everything. The truth is more interesting. It can't truly understand. It can't know if it's wrong. It has no common sense, no stake in the outcome. It predicts — brilliantly — but it doesn't care. Knowing where AI stops is just as powerful as knowing what it can do. So today, the other half of the story: the limits.

## Money Mindset — "Pay Yourself First"
> Welcome back to Money Mindset. Here's the simplest wealth habit there is — pay yourself first. Before bills, before spending, move a little into savings the moment money lands. Most people save whatever's left… and nothing's ever left. Flip it. Save first, live on the rest. Even a tiny amount, automatic and consistent, quietly builds into something real. Small habit. Huge result.

## History Uncovered — "The Small Moments That Changed Everything"
> Welcome back to History Uncovered. We remember the big battles and the famous names. But history often turns on something tiny — a missed message, a wrong turn, a single stubborn decision. The smallest moments can tip the largest events. Today, we zoom in on those quiet hinges of history… the little things that quietly rewrote the world.

## 15-Minute Kitchen — "Flavour Fundamentals"
> Welcome back to 15-Minute Kitchen. Great flavour doesn't come from fancy ingredients or hours in the kitchen. It comes from four fundamentals. Salt — it wakes up every other flavour; add it as you cook, not just at the end. Acid — a squeeze of lemon, a splash of vinegar, that bright lift that makes a dish feel finished. Heat — a properly hot pan gives you colour, and colour means flavour. And fat — butter or good oil carries flavour and makes everything richer. Salt, acid, heat, fat. Master those four and you'll cook better food, faster, without following a recipe to the letter. Go try one tonight.

## Longevity Brief — "The Daily Habits That Add Years"
> Welcome back to Longevity Brief. Forget the miracle cures. The real longevity secrets are almost boringly simple. Move every day — even a walk. Sleep like it's a priority, because it is. Eat mostly plants. Stay connected to people you love. None of it is glamorous… but done daily, for years, it adds up to a longer, sharper, fuller life. Boring works.

## Dad Code — "Connection Beats Correction"
> Welcome back to Dad Code. When your kid messes up, the instinct is to correct — fast. But here's the code: connection comes first. A child who feels understood listens. A child who feels attacked just defends. So before the lecture… the look, the hug, the 'I get it.' Correct the behaviour, yes — but connect with the kid first. That's the order that actually works.

## Dating Decoded — "The Signals That Actually Matter"
> Welcome back to Dating Decoded. We obsess over the wrong signals — texts, timing, games. The signals that actually matter are quieter. Do they listen? Do their actions match their words? Do you feel calmer around them, or more anxious? Chemistry is loud, but consistency is what lasts. Today, we learn to read the signs that actually predict something real.

## Stoic Minute — "Control What You Can"
> Welcome to Stoic Minute. The Stoics built everything on one idea: some things are up to us, and some aren't. Your effort, your attitude, your response — yours. Other people, the outcome, the past — not yours. Suffering comes from gripping what we can't control. Peace comes from letting it go… and pouring everything into what we can. Control what you can. Release the rest. That's your minute.

# SECTION C — DAD CODE: THE MEMOIR SEASON
### Based on "The Frequency of Being Real" by Jamie Wigg

## Ep. 1 — "The Boy Who Spoke in Numbers"
> I always knew I was different. I couldn't name it then — it was just a quiet hum beneath everything else. I grew up one of seven kids; on paper, it was enough. But school broke something in me. Reading and writing weren't just hard… they were walls. Numbers, though? Numbers never laughed at me. By nine I knew my twelve times tables, while words stayed locked behind a gate. So I went quiet. I stopped raising my hand. And in that silence, I learned to read something else — people. Their eyes. Their voices. The things they never said out loud. Back then it felt like survival. Looking back… it was the first frequency I ever learned to hear. This is my story. Welcome to Dad Code.

**Description:** Jamie's story begins — growing up dyslexic, finding refuge in numbers, and learning to read people when words wouldn't come.

## Ep. 2 — "Naming Her Indyanna"
> When my daughter was born, I gave her a name that carried my whole heart — Indyanna. I named her after my mum. There's a memory I hold like something sacred: Christmas Eve, my mum cooking all night, then sitting beside me on the couch, pulling a blanket over us, putting on an Indiana Jones film, and talking softly until I fell asleep. Just her voice and the warmth. It's the most tender memory I have of her. So when my girl arrived, I made sure that love would never leave the room. My only fear wasn't whether I could love her… it was whether I'd pass on my struggles. But the first time she looked up at me and smiled — I knew. This was mine. And I wasn't the boy left out anymore. I was the man holding the centre.

**Description:** Becoming a father, and naming his daughter after his mum — a memory of love passed from one generation to the next.

## Ep. 3 — "The Gift That Said You Can"
> Four children under four. Let that sink in. After the twins came Jackson — and when he arrived, nearly a kilo bigger than the rest, something in my chest just… melted. Two boys, two girls. Balance. I'd spent my whole childhood believing I'd never have this, and now I was drowning in nappies, bottles, and noise — and I loved it. Was it chaos? Completely. One Sunday I left the tap running and flooded the entire bathroom. I rang Vanessa in a panic. She just said, 'Clean it up.' That's the job. But in the middle of all that overwhelm, Jackson's little face felt like a message I needed to hear my whole life: You can do this. This is your gift. I wasn't broken. I was becoming.

**Description:** Four kids under four, beautiful chaos, and the moment Jackson's arrival told Jamie what he'd always needed to hear: you can do this.
