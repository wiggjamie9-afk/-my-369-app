# 📱 Social Media — Dad's Code & The Frequency of Being Real

Two accounts, both built on Jamie's real story. Make a matching **Instagram + TikTok** for each (same name/bio on both).

**Format that works:** vertical video, 20–45 sec, BIG captions (people watch on mute), strong hook in the first 2 seconds. Use your own voice/clips from the podcast episodes.

⚠️ For clips touching mental health, add support lines to the caption (Lifeline 13 11 14 · Beyond Blue 1300 22 4636 · MensLine 1300 78 99 78) and keep the tone hopeful, never sensational.

### Clip 1 — "The vow"
**Hook (first 2 sec):** "When I was ten, I made a vow that changed how I parent."
**Script:** "My dad worked away. His love arrived as money, not presence. Sitting in his car once or twice a year, I had nothing to say and neither did he. So I promised myself — if I ever have kids, I'll be there. Not just in the room. Fully. First football kicks. First dance lessons. Every assembly. Work can wait. Their moments can't."
**Caption:** Being present beats being perfect every single time. 👨‍👧‍👦 #fatherhood #dadlife #presentover perfect #parentingtips #dadsoftiktok

### Clip 2 — "Four under four"
**Hook:** "Four kids under four. Here's what nobody tells you."
**Script:** "Two boys, two girls, all under four. People said 'how do you cope?' Truth is, I didn't always. I once left the tap running and flooded the whole bathroom. I rang my wife in a panic — she just said 'clean it up.' That's the job. It's chaos. But it's the best thing I've ever done."
**Caption:** The chaos IS the good bit. 😅 #dadlife #parenting #twins #bigfamily

### Clip 3 — "The air cuddle"
**Hook:** "My son says two words every night that heal my whole day."
**Script:** "Every night, Jackson whispers 'I love you, Dad.' Then we press our ears together — we call it the air cuddle. No words. Just warmth. And every hard day just… dissolves. That's the feeling I never had as a kid. Now it's alive between us."
**Caption:** This is everything. 💛 #fatherandson #dadlife #bepresent

**Hook:** "I was nine and couldn't read a sentence out loud — but I knew my 12 times tables."
**Script:** "Words were walls. Numbers never laughed at me. So I went quiet, stopped putting my hand up. And in that silence I learned to read something else — people. Their eyes. Their voices. The things they never said. It felt like survival back then. Now I know it was a gift."

## Posting plan (keep it sustainable)
- **2–3 clips per week per account** to start (not daily — don't burn out).
- Batch-film several in one sitting using your podcast scripts.
- Post the same clip to both Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- Always end with: "Full episode on [show name] — link in bio."
